# Episode 0114 — Surprise-gated anchors: mobility priced by evidence passes the wall (2026-08-23)

The language road's next step after 0112, run the same weekend:
design 0019's constraint 2 named a candidate mechanism for the
measured tension between mobility (which exceptions require) and
lawful parity (which three step-priced editions could not keep).
This topic built it and read all three bars in one day: a per-act
prediction-error EMA beside a global one, the anchor row moving
only while `E_a > 2·G` — lawful acts converge below the gate and
freeze themselves; exceptional acts stay above it and keep their
mobility.

What measured true: **Bar SG0 PASS** — gated mobility at m=3 reads
11.692 / 15.077 / 22.769 (pred_late 0.566) against the frozen
thresholds, at the frozen arm's own level (15.538), where step-
priced mobility bracketed 10.2–10.6 across three editions; the gate
opened a median of 23.5 times per 13,000-step lawful life. **Bar
SG2 first edition FAILED as registered** and its reversal routed
the amendment: the frozen control absorbed value-only exceptions
(irr/reg 1.573 — a wrong position on the claimed dial dilutes into
one of twelve probed channels and the shared tensors absorb the
mean shift), so Amendment 1, journaled with those numbers before
any amended arm, made exceptions break the SLOT — the act claims
dial d, the world moves dial d̂ — undilutable, and still inside the
anchor space. On the amended world: **SG2′ PASS** (the control
fails the clause 21/24, irr/reg 2.379, its lawful transfer intact)
and **SG1′ PASS 24/24 on both clauses** — the gated arm absorbs
slot-breaking exceptions at 1.559× its own lawful error (max
1.905) with untried ≡ practiced at 1.002 (max 1.154), the gate
opening ~1,100 times exactly where the world breaks its rules. All
claims [measured]; the honest number beside the ratios: the gated
arm's absolute lawful error runs ~1.8× the frozen control's (0.110
vs 0.060) — the mobility premium persists, stated, unchased.

What was refuted: 0112's mobility wall as a structural limit — the
tension was pricing, not architecture: same anchors, same learning
rate, the gate alone differs, and the wall is gone. Also the first
edition of the exception world: value-only irregulars cannot
discriminate mechanisms because channel-averaging hides them —
exception difficulty must break the slot (Amendment 1's lesson,
now part of the protocol inheritance).

What it taught / what it opened: the gate's economy IS the
mechanism — two orders of magnitude between lawful moves (~23) and
exception-world moves (~1,100), evidence deciding where plasticity
is spent; the same shape as earned persistence one level down. The
absolute mobility premium (~1.8× lawful error) is the named door
for whoever builds the production mechanism (gate hysteresis,
per-slot gates, or post-move re-freezing are the obvious
candidates); the dials (α = 0.1, θ = 2) were declared and never
swept. Design [0019](../02-DESIGN/0019-factored-actions.md) is
updated in place: constraint 2 now carries its measured mechanism,
and the design's definition-of-done items 1–2 are met — the
teacher-world experiment's action side is no longer hypothetical.

Reversal condition: if a body-scale or teacher-world registration
shows the gate failing to separate regimes outside the dial world
(lawful parity lost, or exceptions unabsorbed, on a fresh
registered protocol), constraint 2 reopens and hierarchical or
per-slot mechanisms take its place; if a successor closes the
mobility premium, 0019's caveat updates with its numbers.
