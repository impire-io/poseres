# Conversion visibility — can the brain feel a good trade?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

Episode 0086 named the structural limit: the completion itch and the
recipe extraction key on the pocket *count*, so a value-preserving —
let alone count-losing — trade is invisible or repulsive to them. A
brain that measures wealth by counting cannot want to trade up, value
quality over quantity, or use money. The design bet this topic tests:
the fix is a **wiring choice, not new machinery**. `pocket_index` has
always been anatomy knowledge injected at the constructor; the body
can publish a sensed **pocket-worth** channel (Σ count·worth against
the world's price truth — interoception, the felt-meal precedent) and
the shipped classes can key on *worth* instead of *count* with zero
source changes. If worth-keying restores sight, conversion-blindness
was never a mechanism gap — it was a sense the body hadn't grown. If
it fails, the gap is real and the successor is named with numbers.

## The question

In a trade economy (3 crystal → 1 gem: the count drops, the worth
jumps), does the count-keyed machinery measurably fail — unable even
to *remember* the trade lesson — and does pointing the same shipped
machinery at a sensed worth channel restore learning, election, and
composition of the trade?

## Instrument (frozen with the bars)

  *expected to fail* — that failure is CV1's measured evidence, and

  **V-worth** — the identical shipped classes with
  `pocket_index = the worth channel` for both `RecipeMemory`
  extraction and the policy's completion itch. No other difference.
- **Pilot** seeds 1–8, both arms, published before any 24-seed arm.

## Pre-registered bars

- **Bar CV1 — the blindness is measured, not assumed:** count-keyed
  extraction returns no recipe for lesson B (the trade cannot be
  remembered), and the V-count arm produces gems in ≤ **2/24**
  seeds.
- **Bar CV2 — worth restores sight:** V-worth seeds with ≥ 1 gem ≥
  **18/24**, trade-led (first gem while B is selected, after
  A-attributed crystals) in ≥ half of the gem-earning seeds.
- **Bar CV3′ — no collapse of ordinary acquisition** *(amended
  openly pre-arms; JOURNEY 2026-08-10 has the raw numbers: the
  frozen form compared raw medians, but the worth arm spends three
  crystals per trade — consumption is the economy working, not a
  regression)*: V-worth's median crystal acquisition ≥ **half** of
  V-count's median.

## Reversal condition

If CV2 fails with the instrument verified honest (worth channel
correct, B extracted under worth-keying, pointer advancing), then
re-wiring the existing seams is insufficient: conversion-visibility
is a genuine mechanism gap, and a dedicated worth-delta term in the
completion rule (a source change, spec'd via the usual lifecycle)
becomes the named successor — with these numbers as its motivation.
