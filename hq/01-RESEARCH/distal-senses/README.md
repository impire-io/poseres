# Distal senses — can the body see enough of the world to forage in it?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-13

## Abstract

The head reading (native-survival, HEAD-READING.md) measured that the
taught brain's knowledge is locally TRUE and globally empty: it knows
the block it touches and nothing else exists for it. The anatomy's
entire distal apparatus is three booleans about one column. The owner's
diagnosis: the difference from a human playing Minecraft is the ability
to see what is around — food on the ground, melons at a distance, the
world's structure. This topic adds two instrument-grade senses in the
033 grammar (properties + appearance signatures, egocentric geometry,
no names, no classifiers) and asks whether the seen world makes
foraging learnable where the keyhole world provably did not.

## The question

With a drops sense (nearest ground item: presence, egocentric bearing,
distance, count, signature) and a glance (eight egocentric sectors of
feet-level distance-to-surface plus that surface's signature), does the
taught composition forage — dig food it can only know distally, collect
the seen drop, eat when hungry — in the live world, under the
live-only evidence rule?

## Scope, declared

- **Two senses, appended after `grid`** (every existing offset
  unchanged): `drops` (8) and `glance` (32 = 8 sectors × [distance,
  sig0..2], feet-level center-rays to 16 blocks, 1.0 = open). Both
  egocentric: sectors and bearings rotate with the body's own yaw —
  a turn moves the world across the senses, which is what makes
  walk-toward-the-seen learnable. Survival-mode instrument (obs
  33 → 73); ships only on promotion, like the mouth.
- **The fake bridge carries the SHAPE only** (adapter code-path tests
  — the live-only doctrine): glance computed from its voxel sketch,
  drops permanently empty (the sketch has no item entities, declared).
- Teaching must exercise the senses (walk to a seen melon, collect a
  seen drop); the old taught brain is obsolete (its obs is 33) and is
  NOT migrated — reteach from birth.

## Pre-registered bars (live world only)

- **Bar D1 (the senses are real — instrument bar, before any
  teaching):** standing at the patch, the glance reports the adjacent
  melon at the correct sector and distance with the melon block's
  signature, and rotates sectors under turn commands; a freshly dug
  drop appears in the drops channel with a bearing that tracks body
  turns and a distance that shrinks as the body walks toward it.
  Measured live and published.
- **Bar D2 (the seen forage):** after sense-using teaching, a
  hungry-born life completes ≥ 5 full seen-forage chains in ≤ 25,000
  steps: dig a melon ≥ 2 blocks from the stand (knowable only through
  the glance), collect its drop, genuinely eat — AND the completion
  watch fires with realized pocket gains (the first live completions;
  the deficit gate finally has a bell that rings).
- **Bar D3 (the gate differential, miniature N3):** hungry-born
  gate-on vs gate-off short lives (same taught brain, same world,
  ≥ 3 lives per arm): the gated arm eats more or first-eats sooner in
  every paired comparison. The 042 gate's first live differential.
- **Reading R-patch (recorded, not a bar):** dwell spread across the
  three patches and unique columns — does seeing re-open exploration.

## Reversal condition

If D1 cannot be met (the senses cannot be made honest at the bridge),
the instrument returns to design. If D1 passes but D2 fails with the
senses demonstrably live in the observation, then seeing is not the
missing piece — the reframe's premise (sensory poverty explains the
parked stomach) is refuted for this composition, and the finding goes
back to the design conversation un-spun: the gap is in the machinery
after all, and new machinery needs its own registration.
