# Contested worlds — when a second body shares the world, is an attribution sense necessary and sufficient to keep that world learnable?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-16

## Abstract

Every life so far has been solitary: one body, one world, every change in
the world caused by that body or by the world's own dynamics. The moment a
second body enters, the world changes *for reasons the first body cannot
see* — and by the senses-first rule the suspected remedy is a sense, not a
mechanism: an others/attribution channel carrying presence, egocentric
geometry, and per-agent appearance signatures (Doc 0012 grammar; properties,
never names). This topic tests that suspicion the house way: first measure
whether an invisible peer actually costs learnability (the control), then
whether the sense recovers it, then whether world-side coordination
artifacts — not brain machinery — turn contention into something the bodies
can resolve. A decisive answer unlocks (or closes) the entire multi-body
line: bonds, roles, and population worlds all presuppose this rung. Scale
(hundreds of bodies) is explicitly *not* this topic — it is a named
successor, gated on these verdicts.

## The question

Does a second body in the same world measurably degrade the first body's
event-head learnability, and does a world-derived attribution sense
(presence + egocentric geometry + appearance signature, aggregate grain)
recover it — including discriminating *which* peer is acting?

## The ladder

Four rungs, pre-registered in order; each rung runs only after the previous
has a verdict. Rung 4 carries no bars here — it is the gate to a successor
topic.

1. **The invisible peer (control).** Paired life vs solo baseline, no peer
   sense. Establishes whether the problem is real and gives the number
   everything else must beat.
2. **The attribution sense.** Add the others/attribution channel per
   Doc 0012 (instrument bars before behavior bars: a D1-style live reading
   proves sectors rotate, bearings track, signatures are stable *before*
   any learnability claim).
3. **Contention and the world-side artifact.** Two bodies homing on the
   same scarce food source; then a Minecraft-native claim/coordination
   artifact introduced *in the world*, sensed and actuated like any other
   world fact. No negotiation machinery in the brain.
4. **Scale (gate only).** Population dynamics, convention emergence,
   territory — a successor topic (`population-worlds`), opened only if
   rungs 1–3 pass and a pair has become boring because it is understood.

## Pre-registered bars

Thresholds are pre-registered judgment picks, chosen before any experiment
runs; amending one later happens only openly, with the degenerate raw
numbers recorded in JOURNEY.md. Common protocol unless a bar says
otherwise: live Minecraft only (no fake world at any layer), same world
seed and clean-roomed classroom across arms, taught survival-default brain
in the same starting state per arm, ≥3 segments of ≥1,500 steps per arm,
scripted peer(s) driven by deterministic policies so the peer side is
controlled while the subject brain is the variable.

- **Bar 1 — the cost is real (rung 1):** with an invisible scripted peer
  perturbing the shared channels (breaking blocks, taking drops near the
  subject), the subject's event-head prediction error on world-fact
  channels rises ≥25% relative to its solo baseline on the same seed and
  segments. PASS licenses rung 2. FAIL triggers the reversal condition.
- **Bar 2 — instrument-grade sense (rung 2):** D1-style live reading of
  the others channel passes before any behavior claim: correct
  presence/absence at known configurations, bearings track subject turns,
  distances shrink on approach, and the same peer account yields the same
  appearance signature across ≥3 reconnects while two distinct peers yield
  distinct signatures. All checks pass or the rung stops.
- **Bar 3 — recovery (rung 2):** with the attribution sense active, the
  subject's event-head prediction error on the same channels returns to
  within 10% of the solo baseline under the same peer script that produced
  Bar 1's degradation.
- **Bar 4 — signature-conditioned discrimination (rung 2):** with two
  scripted peers running visibly different deterministic policies (e.g.
  one breaks blocks near the subject, one drops food near the subject),
  prediction error conditioned on which signature is present is ≥20% lower
  than the unconditioned error on peer-attributable transitions — i.e. the
  head demonstrably learns *who* predicts *what*.
- **Bar 5 — contention is measurable (rung 3):** two subject bodies homing
  on the same scarce food source complete measurably fewer eat loops each
  than the solo control on the same source (any statistically clear drop
  across ≥3 paired segments; the raw counts are the record).
- **Bar 6 — the world artifact is elected, and it pays (rung 3):** with a
  world-side claim artifact available (sensed and actuated as ordinary
  world facts, its use taught the way any sense is taught, never forced at
  decision time), at least one body *elects* claim-respecting behavior
  outside teaching, and paired eat completions recover ≥50% of the Bar 5
  gap versus the no-artifact paired arm.

## Reversal condition

- A rung-1 reading in which the invisible peer's perturbations leave the
  subject's prediction error within noise of the solo baseline (relative
  rise <10% across all segments) reverses the topic's premise: other
  bodies would not be a learnability problem, an attribution sense would be
  unlicensed by the senses-first rule, and the topic graduates abandoned
  with that number as the result.
- A rung-2 result where Bar 2 passes but Bars 3–4 both fail (the sense is
  instrument-grade yet recovers nothing) reverses the "a sense is enough"
  premise and sends the topic back to design before any new machinery is
  proposed.
- Standing guard, written now because this topic will invite it: the
  anthropomorphic reading ("they're fighting", "they're friends")
  convinces; the telemetry refutes. No claim about what the bodies are
  doing to each other enters JOURNEY.md without a channel reading behind
  it.
