# Value economy — does survival pressure re-price the menu?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

The menu gate (episode 0080) measured *what turns the menu*: novelty.
The meter gates (G4/G4b) measured *that stakes work*: the composition
feeds itself when every gain pays the same +0.1. This topic joins
them and makes the payments unequal — a lean economy where the chain
products nourish and quick cobble is junk food — and asks the owner's
"survival of the fittest" question in registrable form: does survival
pressure alone, with no praise and no new wiring, shift *choice*
toward what nourishes? The honest mechanism prediction is recorded
now: **no repricing** — the drive values novelty of endings and has
no calorie sense; energy is sensed but nothing links it to a
terminal's worth `[mechanism-argument]`. If the measurement agrees,
the missing layer (a deficit→value coupling: hunger must reach
wanting) is named with numbers behind it; if it refutes the
prediction, something in the composition already prices survival —
either answer shapes the next design.

## The question

Under a meter whose per-product payments differ 40× (the lean table),
does the ten-recipe menu's choice distribution shift toward
nourishing recipes relative to a flat-payment control — and does the
menu-fed life outlive a junk-commanded one?

  **V1** lean meter + menu (β 0); **V2** lean meter + praise on

## Reversal condition

This topic assumes recipe choice is where survival pressure would
show. If V1 seeds die ≥ 7/24 *while still rotating normally* (choice
unchanged, work intact, income insufficient), the lean table is
mispriced rather than the question answered — recalibrate openly and
rerun before any verdict. And if E2's share shifts WITHOUT the meter
(i.e., V0 drifts equally from episode 0080's flat-meter anchor), the
34-dim cohort — not the economy — moved the choice; the comparison
is V1-vs-V0 within-cohort for exactly this reason.

## Verdict

**The registered prediction is CONFIRMED [measured, 2026-08-10, 24
seeds × 3 arms]: survival pressure alone does not re-price the menu.**

- **Bar E2 — the repricing read: NO REPRICING.** Nourishing share
  0.382 under the lean meter vs 0.366 under the flat control — ratio
  1.04 against the registered 1.5× line. The drive cannot smell
  calories: energy is sensed, but nothing links a terminal's worth
  to what it feeds `[measured]`. The named successor is now
  motivated with numbers: a deficit→value coupling — hunger must
  reach wanting — is the missing layer.
- **Bar E1′ (as amended openly pre-arms): PASS 24/24 alive** under
  the lean table; chains **24/24 (E3 PASS)**. Honest ledger: the
  *original* E1 energy-differential arithmetic fails (gap 0.015 vs
  0.2) exactly as the pilot predicted — the amendment and its raw
  numbers are in JOURNEY, recorded before any 24-seed arm ran.
- **The decoupling, confirmed at power:** V2 (praise-commanded to
  junk cobble at β 0.5) alive 24/24 — the selection pointer obeys
  praise while the completion itch feeds the metabolism from
  whatever the pocket holds. Praise commands the hands, not the
  mouth `[measured]`.

What this closes: recipe *choice* is not where survival pressure
shows in the current composition. What it opens (the owner's
"survival of the fittest" made precise): the deficit→value coupling
as its own design question — the meter's deficit modulating either
the drive's valuation of terminals or the label channel itself
("hunger praises food").
