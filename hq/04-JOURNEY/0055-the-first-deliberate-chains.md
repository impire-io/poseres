E2.0, the dwell gate, ran the same day it was registered — the cheapest
falsification of the goal-object idea, at the policy seam, zero src edits.
`GoalBiasPolicy` mirrors the shipped curiosity lookahead exactly, plus
λ · (−distance of the predicted observation to a teacher-given goal
snapshot: standing at the workshop, facing the wood). Subjects were the
E1 graduates; the frozen bar demanded **holding** — ≥ 18/24 seeds spending
≥ 20% of a 5,000-step window within Chebyshev 2 of the wood column, with

**The gate FAILed at power [measured]: 0/24 seeds reached 20% dwell.** No
λ in the frozen grid {0.25, 1, 4} cleared the pilot floor (medians
4.6% / 4.2% / 5.6%); the confirmatory ran at the best (λ = 4) and read
median dwell **3.8% vs frontier's 0.3%**. The pull is real — mean distance
to the workshop halves, unique positions halve — but it is **orbit, not
hold**: exactly the pre-named failure mode (a one-step obs-distance has no
gradient out on the featureless plain), dominating once ε and drive noise
leak the runner off the plateau. Per the registered decision rule and
episode 0054's reversal, **the λ-on-one-step-lookahead form is refuted as
a holding mechanism, and the goal-object approach pauses before any src
build.** The frozen prediction (PASS at λ ≥ 1) was wrong, and the bar did
its job against the author's own expectation.

**And the context rows carry the day [measured]: the first deliberate
crafting chains in the project's history.** Across all 42 goal-biased
runs: **two full log→planks→sticks chains** (seed 6 at λ 0.25; seed 7 at
λ 4, deterministic across pilot and confirmatory) and a dug log or better
in ~10 runs — every log 12 consecutive deliberate dig ticks, planks and
sticks vanilla-exact staging-grid crafts, with dozens of post-craft
stage/unstage cycles showing sustained engagement. Against this:
frontier-alone has never produced a single log anywhere in the measured
record — 328,560 live c1c steps (E0), 24 E1 free-runs, 26 F arms today.
**E1's knowledge was deployable all along; it needed pull, not push.**
Wanting-without-holding touches the workshop rarely; when it touches, the
taught chain executes. The topic's two nulls and this existence proof now
compose: drives alone make contact but no sequences (E0); knowledge
without wanting is not behavior (E1); knowledge plus even a failing want
yields the first directed sequences (E2.0) [measured, all three].
