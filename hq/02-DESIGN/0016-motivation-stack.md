## The theses the arc measured

1. **Layers compose; none suffices alone.** Every single-layer gate
   nulled or split; the blessed stack that lives (design 0015) is
   layers 0–5 working together.
2. **Every layer's twin is real and arrives on schedule.** The
   hangover (5), perseveration (4, closed by the boundary), the
   miser's inverse — welfare (1, the provisioning purse), the gate's
   inversion (2).
3. **Sensation beats valuation at the seam.** The flood (a sense)
   measurably initiates; the deficit gate (a value warp) inverted.
   Engineering belongs at the body seam; the cortex stays generic.
