# Partial-gap world — does renewal slower than joint consumption open a middle between no war and total war?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-18

## Abstract

Lean-worlds (episode 0110) ended on a knife edge: contests over
fast, non-renewable food are all-or-nothing — 2 melons is total loss
(hostile below-12 steady-state 1.000), 3 melons is no loss for
anyone (0.000), and nothing tested lands between. Design
[0017](../../02-DESIGN/0017-lean-worlds.md) names the gate every
follow-up must find first: a declared world config whose blind-brain
hostile reading is a *partial* gap — below-12 steady-state in
0.10–0.90. This topic hunts that world on the design's axis 2,
generalizing the calibration walk from larder *size* to **renewal
rate**: regrowth slower than joint consumption, so contests repeat
and per-encounter loss is partial. Finding it unlocks axis 1 (the
peer-taught brain, the sense's outcome license) as a successor
topic; not finding it extends the all-or-nothing verdict to a second
axis and measures axis 2's renewal arm down.

## The question

Does a declared renewal rate — melons regrowing slower than a
subject and a hostile peer jointly consume them — produce a world
whose hostile-86 below-12 steady-state lands in 0.10–0.90, where the
larder-size axis produced only 0.000 or 1.000?

## Pre-registered bars

Common protocol, inherited from 0110 unchanged: live Minecraft only,
tick rate 100 / 50 ms, engine seed 1, blessed stack exactly (the
default 86/13 survival body, v2.2.0), hungry-born free roam, one
life = 3 segments × 67 cycles × 75 steps (15,075 steps), views
persisted, every peer act logged. **Meter window:** segments 2–3
only (the hungry-born transient excluded, 0110's registered
operationalization); "below-12" = fraction of window steps with
food < 12/20. The hostile peer is 0109/0110's policy, unchanged, at
script speed. "Hostile-86" = the taught 86-dim (blind) brain against
that peer. **The ladder is declared before it runs:** the rungs — a
fixed 2-melon larder (C-1, the total-war rung) plus a renewal source
walked from fast regrowth down toward none, with the renewal
mechanism and each rung's exact config — are recorded in JOURNEY.md
before the walk starts. Renewal rate is this topic's one variable;
patch separation is NOT walked here (see reversal).

- **Bar P0 — the renewing world stands solo:** each candidate rung's
  config supports a solo-86 life with zero starvation health-loss,
  ≥ 3 detected eats, and steady-state below-12 ≤ 0.10. A rung that
  fails solo is disqualified regardless of its hostile reading — the
  gap must be the peer's doing, not the world's.
- **Bar P1 — the partial gap exists (headline):** some declared
  rung, its config frozen before its hostile arm runs, yields
  hostile-86 below-12 steady-state in **0.10–0.90** (one life, the
  common protocol). PASS freezes that config as *the partial-gap
  world* — design 0017's definition-of-done item 1.
- **Bar P2 — the gap replicates:** a second hostile-86 life on the
  frozen config, same protocol and same taught brain, lands in
  0.10–0.90 again. One life per arm was 0110's journaled confound;
  the gate any successor builds on gets two lives, not one.

## Reversal condition

- **No declared rung lands in the band** — every renewal rate reads
  either no war (< 0.10) or total war (> 0.90) after Bar P0
  screening: the all-or-nothing verdict generalizes from larder size
  to renewal rate. The topic graduates abandoned carrying the second
  null on axis 2's cheapest arm; the remaining candidates from
  design 0017 are spatially separated patches (a fresh registration,
  not an amendment here — one variable per topic) and
  comparable-speed bodies (axis 3).
- **P2 fails after P1 passes** (the band reading does not
  replicate): the "partial gap" was between-life variance, not
  world structure — no config freezes, and the finding is that one
  life cannot read this band. Any continuation must register a
  multi-life protocol first.
- Standing guard, inherited from 0109/0110: no claim about what the
  bodies are doing to each other enters JOURNEY.md without a channel
  reading or an act log behind it.
