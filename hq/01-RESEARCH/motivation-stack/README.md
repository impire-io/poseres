# Motivation stack — which minimal layer set produces election?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-08

## Abstract

The direction hypothesis that five measured nulls and one owner
conversation drew, promoted from a loose note into the topic that
orders the goals arc. The claim: motivation is not one mechanism but a
**stack** — budget, deficits, option-value, completion itch, borrowed
goals, imagination — each layer patching the degenerate twin of the
one below, and every null in the record so far is a single upper
layer tested in a creature and world missing the lower floor. The
successor question left by goal-homing (episode 0069) is the topic's
edge: position, knowledge, and contact are all solved or provably
insufficient; **election** — choosing dig₁…dig₁₂ at a place the drive
scores as exhausted — is the isolated wall. A decisive answer here
picks the E2.1 mechanism with evidence instead of taste.

**Provenance:** owner–assistant conversation, 2026-08-08 evening,
immediately after goal-homing's confirmatory fired its middle branch.
The owner supplied the bottom layer via Stephen Fry's *Great Leap
Years* (life as optimization toward the calorie), the
parent/attachment layer, and the option-counting frame. Everything
below is `[judgment]` unless tagged; measured mappings cite episodes.

## The question

Which minimal set of stack layers, added to a brain that already
holds position (goal-homing's oracle) and knowledge (the 034
demonstrations), produces **election** — the deliberate first rung of
a known chain, sustained to completion — at pre-registered power?

## The stack

**0 · The filter — persist or don't.** Not a goal and not a
mechanism: selection. Nobody sets "survive"; non-survivors are
absent. The regress "efficiency needs a goal needs a motive…" bottoms
out in a filter, not a wish. Consequence: the bottom of the stack is
supplied by the *world*, not the brain.

**1 · The budget.** A running cost of existing (the calorie). Makes
every moment a purchase and all activities commensurable — the
currency layer. *Ugly twin:* the miser — cost without value learns
torpor. *Record:* untested; both testbeds are paradise (idling free,
nothing decays, vitals pinned) — repeatedly implicated as why
nothing commits `[mechanism-argument]` (0068, 0069).

**2 · Deficits.** The budget compiled into sensation: hunger,
fatigue, social hunger. Makes *specific* doors valuable at specific
times; the attachment point teaching needs. *Ugly twin:* monomania.
*Record:* E1 (0054) `[measured]` — 45 demonstrations provably
carried, 0/24 behavior; knowledge found no pre-existing want.

**3 · Option-value.** Value states by what they make possible (a log
is money). Explains acquisition without external goals; needs
layer-6 what-if to count doors. *Ugly twin:* the hoarder — doors
collected, never walked through; commitment is not this layer.
*Record:* untested; a one-variable gate exists on the goal-homing
instrument (clone-step option-count in place of −Φ).

**4 · The completion itch.** Begun sequences pull toward their ends;
micro-commitment carrying a chooser through a chain's *middle*.
*Ugly twin:* sunk-cost perseveration. *Record:* the sharpest current
gap `[measured]` — c1c finished 449 digs in one era then none
(0068); goal-homing held the bot at the workshop 120,000 steps with
zero elections of the 12-tick dig (0069). The body already senses
dig progress; this layer is one term from testable.

**5 · Borrowed goals / the parent.** Attachment as the transmission
channel: goals arrive from others onto a pre-wired social deficit;
the parent's verdict is *sensed, never wired* (the owner's
constitutional line, 2026-07-22). Death-grief gives stakes; scarce
parental attention is a feature, not a bug. *Ugly twins:* the
sycophant (Goodhart on the approval signal) and parent-absence
inertness. *Record:* E3.0 (0057) `[measured]` — approval sensed 45
times, the expectation never formed; sensing is not wanting.
Prerequisite named in 0058: an **event-sensitive predictor**.

**6 · Imagination.** Futures bid on the present, and the bid is
*felt*, not just computed — what-if with evaluation attached (the
E2.1 "rollouts" adjacency). *Ugly twin:* the dreamer — simulation
replacing action. *Record:* one-step lookahead exists and is
insufficient for election `[measured]` (0069); depth and felt
evaluation untested.

**7 · Identity / narrative.** The self-story as a value source.
Beyond current scope; recorded so the map has a top.

## Pre-registered bars

The topic runs as a ladder, the self-set-goals pattern: each gate
below **registers its own bars in this README before its run** (owner
sets or approves thresholds); no gate builds src before its own
registration. Proposed order — the stack's, cheapest-first:

- **G1 — completion-pull** (layer 4): a small value on advancing a
  begun, *sensed* intention (the mining-progress channel), one
  variable on the goal-homing instrument, homing term retained. The
  question: does hold + itch elect dig₁…dig₁₂ and finish chains?
- **G2 — option-value oracle** (layer 3): −Φ replaced by
  option-count(state-after) via clone-step. Does valuing doors elect
  the dig that opens them — and then walk through, or hoard?
- **G3 — event-sensitive predictor** (prerequisite for layer 5;
  0058's named successor): can the brain learn to *expect* a rare
  discrete event at all? Gate on prediction, not behavior.
- **G4 — a world with a meter** (layer 1): idling costs, food
  exists, death at zero. The stakes substrate; the miser watched
  from day one.
- **G5 — approval, revisited** (layer 5): after G3, with the
  sycophancy watch pre-registered.

## G1 pre-registration — the completion-pull gate (REGISTERED 2026-08-08, before any run)

Bars set under the owner's delegated autonomy ("go ahead,
autonomously", 2026-08-08) — recorded as such; the runner does not
exist yet.

**The gate policy.** `HomingItchPolicy` = goal-homing's
`HomingBiasPolicy` with the measured λ\* = 0.25 homing term retained,
plus exactly one new term per candidate action:

```
value(a) = drive_value(pred_a) + 0.25 · (−Φ(pos_after(a)))
                                + κ · (progress_after(a) − progress_now)
```

`progress_after(a)` and `Φ` both read from the one proven clone-step
oracle; `progress_after = 1.0` when the clone completes a dig (the
sensed channel zeroes on the completing tick — inventory gain is the
completion signal), else the clone's sensed mining channel;
`progress_now` = the live sensed mining channel. Same draw order,
ε/maturity gates, and candidate-skip rule as every gate in this arc.
The world's own mechanics give the term its teeth `[measured in
source]`: any non-dig action resets crack progress to zero, so the
term rewards starting (~κ/12), rewards continuing, and penalizes
abandoning in proportion to sunk progress (−κ·p).

**Subjects and protocol.** The 24 P0-proven graduates, H = 5,000,
fresh worlds. Pilot: seeds 1–8 × κ ∈ {0.25, 1.0, 4.0}, published here
before the confirmatory; **κ\*** = smallest κ whose pilot median
completed wood digs ≥ 1 (else best median, grid published).
Confirmatory: 24 seeds at κ\*. Context arm (no bar): itch-only
(κ\*, λ = 0), seeds 1–8 — separates the itch from the hold. Controls
standing from the record: F, G-λ4, and goal-homing's H-λ0.25 arm
(0 logs, 0 chains — the no-itch baseline).

**Frozen bars.**

- **Bar A — the rung: ≥ 18/24 confirmatory seeds gain ≥ 1 `oak_log`**
  (a completed 12-tick wood dig) in-window. The term targets digs
  directly, so the demanding k applies — a weak pass must not launder
  the layer.
- **Bar B — the chain: ≥ 6/24 seeds ≥ 1 full log→planks→sticks
  chain** (the arc's standing k, E1's bar).

**Decision rule (frozen).** A+B PASS → layer 4 + position suffice for
election: the learnable-itch build and E2.1 shaping become the next
registrations. A PASS, B FAIL → the wall moves up one rung, from
digging to *staging/crafting election* (no progress channel exists to
itch on there) — an informative split that reorders G2/G3. A FAIL →
the one-step completion-pull is refuted at oracle bound; G2/G3
proceed.

**Frozen prediction (author's; three predecessors were wrong).**
Bar A passes 20–23/24 — post-start, continuing beats abandoning by
κ·p and only ε interrupts; expected ~39 ticks per log at ε = 0.1.
Bar B is the open one: predicted 4–9/24, on the argument that
log-in-pocket-at-the-workshop is the taught context and the two
existence chains prove the path re-elects — a narrow pass or an
honest miss.

## G1 pilot [measured, 2026-08-08] — published before the confirmatory

8 seeds × κ ∈ {0.25, 1.0, 4.0}, λ = 0.25 hold retained:

- **Every seed digs at every κ**: 8/8 seeds with ≥ 1 oak_log; median
  logs per seed 6 / 9.5 / 9.5 (κ 0.25 / 1 / 4). The no-itch baseline
  (goal-homing H-λ0.25) recorded **zero** logs in 24 runs.
- **The taught first craft re-elects at volume**: 107–274 planks per
  seed — the log→planks loop runs constantly once logs exist.
- **Full chains**: 3/8 seeds at κ 0.25; 4/8 at κ 1 and 4 (sticks up
  to 69). Dwell stays 99.98–100% — the hold is untouched.
- κ 1.0 and 4.0 produce seed-identical rows: past κ ≈ 1 the itch term
  saturates the argmax; **κ\* = 0.25** (smallest with median ≥ 1).
- Ugly-twin watch, live already: seeds 2/4 at κ ≥ 1 dig 107–143
  cobblestone — the itch is target-agnostic and will finish *any*
  crack (mineral digs are 4-tick, cheap). Noted, not pathological at
  pilot scale; the perseveration row rides into the confirmatory.

- **The itch-only context arm (κ\* = 0.25, λ = 0, seeds 1–8): 2/8

  **the pass belongs to the composition**, not to either term alone.
  The stack's core claim (layers compose; single layers fail) is now

- Frozen-prediction ledger: Bar A predicted 20–23, actual 24 (fourth

**Per the frozen decision rule (A+B): layer 4 + position suffice for

## Reversal condition

The map is refuted in the best possible way if any single-layer gate
produces robust repeated chains without its lower layers — the stack
collapses toward that layer and the record simplifies. It is
weakened if compound layers fail the way single ones did. Either
outcome is progress; the map orders the queue, it does not get to
win.
