# Episode 0103 — Native survival: the composition lives on the world's own metabolism (2026-08-11 → 2026-08-16)

The c1e verdict said the scenario, not the architecture, was the
tangent — a hand-dosed synthetic metabolism in a de-natured world.
This topic re-grounded the life in Minecraft's own economy and, five
days and three spawned mechanisms later, answers its question YES:
released into a world whose food regrows by the game's own rules, the
taught composition sustains its own vitals by real foraging — and the
entire harness-meter layer (drain, pay, taper, stipend, steward) is
retired in one move, replaced by the world.
