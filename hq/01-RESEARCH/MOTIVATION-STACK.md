# The motivation stack — a direction hypothesis (2026-08-08)

**What this is:** the map that four measured nulls and one owner
conversation drew, written down so the next arcs inherit it. **What it
is not:** commitments. No layer below enters the code except through
its own registered gate with owner-set bars, per the house method.
Everything here is `[judgment]` unless tagged otherwise; the measured
mappings cite their episodes.

**Provenance:** owner–assistant conversation, 2026-08-08 evening,
immediately after the goal-homing confirmatory fired its middle branch
(hold at 99.98%, chains at zero). The owner supplied the bottom layer
via Stephen Fry's *Great Leap Years* (life as optimization toward the
calorie), the parent/attachment layer, and the option-counting frame;
the conversation assembled them against the record.

## The claim

Motivation is not one mechanism but a stack, each layer patching the
degenerate failure mode ("ugly twin") of the one below. Our measured
nulls line up as single upper layers tested in a creature and world
missing the lower ones — and the stack predicts that pattern continues
until either a lower layer is built or a gate proves a single layer
suffices (which would refute this map and simplify everything).

## The layers, bottom up

**0 · The filter — persist or don't.** Not a goal and not a mechanism:
selection. Nobody sets "survive" as an objective; non-survivors are
absent. The regress "efficiency needs a goal needs a motive…" bottoms
out here, in a filter, not a wish. Consequence for us: the bottom of
the stack is supplied by the *world*, not the brain.

**1 · The budget.** A running cost of existing (the calorie). Makes
every moment a purchase and all activities commensurable — the
currency layer. *Ugly twin:* the miser — cost without value learns
torpor. *Record:* untested; both testbeds are paradise (idling free,
nothing decays, vitals pinned) — repeatedly implicated as why nothing
commits `[mechanism-argument]`, episodes 0068, this arc.

**2 · Deficits.** The budget compiled into sensation: hunger, fatigue,
social hunger. Makes *specific* doors valuable at specific times; the
attachment point teaching needs. *Ugly twin:* monomania — one deficit
drowning the stack. *Record:* E1 (episode 0054) `[measured]`: 45
demonstrations provably carried, 0/24 behavior — knowledge found no
pre-existing want to attach to.

**3 · Option-value.** Value states by what they make possible (a log
is money). Explains acquisition without external goals; needs layer-6
what-if to count doors. *Ugly twin:* the hoarder — doors collected,
never walked through; commitment is not explained by this layer.
*Record:* untested. A one-variable gate exists on the proven
goal-homing instrument (clone-step option-count in place of −Φ).

**4 · The completion itch.** Begun sequences pull toward their ends;
micro-commitment. Carries a chooser through the *middle* of a chain.
*Ugly twin:* sunk-cost perseveration. *Record:* the sharpest current
gap `[measured]`: c1c dug thousands of times and finished 449 digs in
one era then none (0068); goal-homing held the bot at the workshop
120,000 steps with zero elections of the 12-tick dig. The body
already senses dig progress — this layer is one term away from
testable.

**5 · Borrowed goals / the parent.** Attachment as the transmission
channel: goals arrive from others and land on a pre-wired social
deficit; the parent's verdict is *sensed, never wired* (the owner's
constitutional line, 2026-07-22). Death-grief gives stakes; scarcity
of the parent's attention is a feature. *Ugly twins:* the sycophant
(Goodhart on the approval signal) and parent-absence inertness.
*Record:* E3.0 (episode 0057) `[measured]`: approval sensed 45 times,
the expectation never formed — sensing is not wanting. Prerequisite
named in 0058: an **event-sensitive predictor**.

**6 · Imagination.** Futures bid on the present, and the bid is
*felt*, not just computed — what-if with evaluation attached (E2.1's
"rollouts" adjacency). *Ugly twin:* the dreamer — simulation replacing
action. *Record:* one-step lookahead exists and is insufficient for
election `[measured]` (this arc); depth and felt evaluation untested.

**7 · Identity / narrative.** The self-story as a value source.
Beyond current scope; recorded so the map has a top.

## Candidate gates this map orders (none registered)

1. **Event-sensitive predictor** — already the record's named
   successor (0058); prerequisite for any wanting that keys on rare
   events, parental or otherwise.
2. **Completion-pull** (layer 4) — a small value on advancing a begun,
   *sensed* intention (the mining-progress channel exists); one
   variable on the goal-homing instrument. Does hold + itch elect
   dig₁…dig₁₂?
3. **Option-value oracle** (layer 3) — swap −Φ for
   option-count(state-after) on the same instrument; does valuing
   doors elect the dig that opens them — and then walk through, or
   hoard?
4. **A world with a meter** (layer 1) — idling costs, food exists,
   death at zero; the stakes substrate for grief, urgency, and
   efficiency meaning anything. Watch the miser from day one.
5. **Approval, revisited** (layer 5) — after gate 1, with the
   sycophancy watch pre-registered.

## Reversal condition

This map is refuted in the best possible way if any single-layer gate
produces robust repeated chains without its lower layers — e.g.
completion-pull alone chaining at bar — in which case the stack
collapses toward that layer and the record gets simpler. It is
weakened if compound layers fail the same way single ones did. Either
outcome is progress; the map exists to order the queue, not to win.
