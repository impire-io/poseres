# Episode 0101 — The last crack: commitment pays the final stroke (2026-08-15)

The decree diagnostic (episode 0100) left a precise defect: parked at
a melon, the taught head dug 211 of 389 frames, its longest hold
reached mining 0.97 — and no dig ever completed, because vanilla
resets block damage on release. This topic asked why the hold quits
and what pays the final frames, with the mechanism hypothesis (the
itch's clipped horizon) pre-registered — and the measurement overturned
the hypothesis on its way to the answer.

**L1, the attribution** [measured]: the per-frame value trace
acquitted the clip outright — 0 of 4 directed releases in its
predicted band, releases scattered at progress 0.011–0.546 — and
convicted something sharper. The vote is a KNIFE-EDGE: directed
release margins ran 0.069, 0.040, 0.0063, 0.00002 against a hold
margin of only κ·Δ̂ ≈ 0.008, so a 30-frame live dig must win ~30
consecutive near-coin-flips of frame noise; and the ε-gate (0.1) was
killing holds DIG was WINNING — all three explore releases had DIG
ahead on the value table, one at progress 0.994, one frame from the
world's own break. (ε had joined the suspect list in a pre-run
amendment, from reading the code: 0.9³⁰ ≈ 4% survival by chance
alone.)

**The mechanism, built opt-in** (constitution I — defaults bit-exact,
RNG streams included): `commit_kappa` incumbency — repeating the
previous action while sensed progress advances earns 0.1, set above
the largest measured flip margin — plus `explore_defers_holds`, the
ε-gate yielding to a live hold. The first committed arm then measured
commitment's degenerate twin ON SCHEDULE: a 517-frame DIG lock across
breaks — perseveration, the addiction machine — closed by the
intention boundary: a progress collapse (the world's own
completion/reset) clears the incumbent, so incumbency dies with its
intention. 25 policy unit tests hold all three pieces.

**L2, the proof** [measured] (powered to 3 repeats per arm after
7-vs-1 single-arm variance, amendment recorded with the numbers):
baseline 0 breaks in 4,500 parked steps — the shipped vote cannot
finish a live dig in that geometry, ever — committed 10 breaks, 11
collects, and one repeat ran the WHOLE chain: steered contact →
committed dig → break → collect → 6 eats to full satiation (food
8 → 20), **first eat at step 333** — an order of magnitude faster
than anything in the record (previous best 1,119; the flood era
3.8k–5.2k). L3 [measured]: the full gate green with flags off,
byte-frozen suite included.

What it teaches, plainly: the taught skills were never missing — the
motivation layer re-fought a settled decision every frame, and any
noise (the flood's designed intrusion, ε, prediction drift) broke
multi-frame intentions at their thinnest margin. Commitment is the
motivation-stack's missing rung between the itch and the recipe, and
its twin (perseveration) arrived exactly as the stack's thesis
predicts — every layer patches a degenerate twin below and brings its
own. Every "the skill expresses with variance" reading since c1e
inherits this correction. Design:
[0014](../02-DESIGN/0014-the-last-crack.md). Promotion into the
shipped default is a spec-kit feature decision, deliberately not
taken in a research topic.

Reversal condition: if committed free-roaming lives (flags on, no
decree) fail to raise completed digs and eats over the measured
flag-off floors (observable: an N2-style re-run reading break/eat
counts within the old arms' band), commitment-as-carrier reopens; if
committed lives develop measurable perseveration despite the
intention boundary (observable: consecutive same-action runs > 200
frames spanning ≥ 3 progress collapses), the boundary design reopens.
