# Episode 0058 — Self-set goals, parked: five gates and an existence proof (2026-07-22 → 2026-07-24)

The topic began with a friend's question — what does the brain have *as
goals*? curiosity is an inner drive, but does it ever set itself a
target and pursue it? — held against the code, where every deliberate
step is a one-step greedy hill-climb with no held target. Three days
and five registered gates later, the owner parked it. This episode is
the topic's record; the folder retires with it (lifecycle: abandoned;
in substance: parked, results standing).

**The ladder, every rung decided by its pre-registered bar:**

- **E0 — the premise** [measured]: frontier-alone produced 0 planks, 0
  sticks, 0 chains — and not one log — in 328,560 live steps. Drives
  make contact with the mechanism, never sequences through it. (E0b:
  anti-idle GREEN, idle 3.1%; frontier vindicated for Minecraft.)
- **E1 — teaching alone, feature 034** [measured]: 45 demonstrations
  provably carried (19 vs 13 frames) and taught 0/24 vs the ≥ 6/24 bar.
  Knowledge without wanting is not behavior — and the mechanism reading
  cut deeper: a realized-progress drive scores a mastered lesson at ~0,
  so **the graduate leaves the workshop because the teaching worked.**
- **E2.0 — the λ dwell gate** [measured]: a goal-biased lookahead
  (λ·(−distance to a taught goal observation)) FAILs holding at 0/24 —
  orbit, not hold — while its context rows deliver **the project's
  first deliberate chains**: 2 full log→planks→sticks across 42 runs,
  vs zero from frontier-alone anywhere in the record.
- **E2.0h — the horizon read** [measured]: C(H) flat at 1/24 from
  H = 5,000 to 40,000. Chains are decided in the **departure window**
  (both recorded chains < 700 ticks in); past the λ term's capture
  radius the one-step obs-distance is flat and no runner ever returned.
  A floor, not a rate; the λ form exhausted for holding *and* rate.
- **E3.0 — the verdict-sensation gate** [measured]: satisfaction in its
  constitutional form (approval as a *sensed* event, never a reward
  wire — the owner's line, recorded on the way in). The pulse provably
  reached the senses 45 times; the expectation of it never reliably
  formed (specific 14/24 vs the ≥ 18 bar, 5 seeds anti-predictive at
  the exact tick approval always arrives), and behavior landed on the
  frozen prediction exactly: V = M = 0/24. Sensing is not wanting, from
  the approval side; the refutation is predictor-shaped, not
  concept-shaped [judgment].

**Refuted along the way:** rev.2's landscape claim (demonstration makes
the goal the highest-frontier option — it does during teaching and
silences it after); the orbit-and-touch hope; the memoryless chain-rate
model; the author's frozen predictions at three consecutive gates in
one day — the bars, not the intuition that set them, decided every
rung. Twice a lost scratchpad instrument was rebuilt bit-faithfully
from the committed record alone and proven by replication gates (E2.0h
P0: median dwell 3.76% vs recorded 3.8%, chain set exact) — the
pre-registration discipline paying for itself in kind.

**What stands [measured]:** knowledge + even a weak, failing want
produced the only deliberate multi-step behavior in the project's
history; nothing else — not drives, not teaching, not patience, not
sensed approval — moved the hands at all. **The answer as of parking
[judgment]:** the brain does not set goals for itself, and no cheap
seam-level want converts taught knowledge into reliable directed
behavior. The missing machinery is real and named: a goal term with
gradient far from the goal (E2.0b, the unexplored door), an
event-sensitive predictor (E3's named successor), and multi-step reach
(the one-step wall behind every rung). None is built; none is licensed
without its own registered gate.

Reversal condition: the topic reopens if (a) the c1c run — the standing
watch — ever produces a multi-step crafting chain on its own (the
premise weakens: directed behavior without goal machinery), or (b) the
owner picks E2.0b or an event-sensitive predictor design, either of
which must pre-register its gate and bars before any run. Until then
the existence proof is the topic's standing result.
