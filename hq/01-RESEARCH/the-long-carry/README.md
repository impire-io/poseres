# The long carry — does composition pay where the chain outruns the scaffold?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-26

## Abstract

Episode 0119 closed the relocated comparison: on live Minecraft's
position-opaque forage world, composition in either shape is
measured-indistinguishable from flat — because the probe world's
chains are short (~60–100 steps) under heavy policy scaffolding, wild
behavior is carried by the recipe/itch stack and the event head, and
the deep-sequence demand that motivated the compositional gate never
binds those meters. The honest state of the gate after 0118 + 0119:
no tested shape has demonstrated a composition win anywhere except a
lab-interior shift that did not transfer, and the deciding arena — a
position-opaque LONG-HORIZON world, chains beyond the policy
scaffolding's reach — does not exist. This topic builds that arena on
the survival rig's live-measurement standard (real server, real
mineflayer body, real food economy; nothing faked), proves it binds
before any comparison reads, and runs the registered
flat/tower/bind-pred comparison there. Design 0020 carries the
caution this topic exists to resolve: do not build the gate's shape
into the kernel until this protocol decides. The 0119 composed
scaffolding is the starting rig, resurrected from git history
(commit 4171779: `compose.py` with the sidecar, `opaque_runner.py`,
`snap_test.py`) — live-real at C1 scale and lifecycle-benign as
measured there.

## The question

On a live world that hides chain position AND whose chains outrun the
policy scaffolding's reach — where the life must carry where-it-is
rather than sense it or be walked through it one step at a time —
does a composed tier pay beyond spread at last, and if so which shape
carries: the stacked tower or the identity-bound reference?

## Reversal condition

If no candidate world passes H0 after honest attempts — the memory
gap cannot be manufactured on the live rig at payable cost — the
topic abandons or re-scopes openly with the attempts on the record,
and 0020's caution (do not build the gate's shape into the kernel)
stands indefinitely, with "arena unconstructible at C1 scale" as the
recorded reason. If the flat arm matches its sibling on the opaque
world (no gap at H0(c)), the scaffold's reach is longer than 0119
measured — the chain escalates (length, aliased decision points)
pre-run, never mid-comparison. And the standing conditions this
topic exists to test, from 0119: a beyond-spread composition win
here reopens the gate's shape question with these numbers and this
world; a composition loss on a valid arena is the strongest evidence
yet that the composed-frame prototype does not buy carried state,
and 0020's open question moves from WHERE composition pays to
WHETHER this mechanism can.
