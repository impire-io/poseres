# Learned worth — can the body learn its own price book?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

Episode 0088 restored trade sight by pointing `pocket_index` at a
worth channel — but the world *published* the prices. A brain headed
for markets must learn what things are worth from experience. The
minimal honest grounding is already in the stack: **worth is what
feeds you**. The body keeps a per-item price book, updated
interoceptively from its own felt meals (the meter's pay at each
gain), and publishes the worth channel as Σ count · *learned* price
— born ignorant, converging toward the world's metabolic truth as
the body eats. The teachers matter once more: the demonstrations are
where the body first *tastes* each item (the taught trade is the
first gem meal), so lessons bootstrap the price book exactly as they
bootstrap the head. The gate asks whether 0088's result survives
the move from published truth to learned estimate.

## The question

With prices learned (per-item EMA of felt pay, α = 0.25; the book is
body state carried across lessons — the world resets, the tongue
does not) and never published, does the worth-keyed machinery still
learn, elect, and compose the trade — and does the book converge to
the metabolic truth?

## Pre-registered bars

- **Bar LW1 — ignorance and blindness measured:** the book is born
  all-zero (instrument assertion) AND the count-keyed floor stays
  blind: lesson B unextractable, gems ≤ **2/24**.
- **Bar LW2 — the learned sense works:** worth-keyed-on-learned
  gems ≥ **18/24**, trade-led in ≥ half of the gem-earning seeds —
  0088's shape achieved with no published truth anywhere.
- **Bar LW3 — the book converges:** among gem-earning L1 seeds at

  truth (1.0) and the median learned crystal price within ±20% of
  0.1 (per-seed books published).

## Reversal condition

If LW2 fails while the book is verified converged (LW3's condition
met on the taught cohort) and extraction verified, then felt-EMA
grounding is insufficient even with a correct book — the failure is
in the *dynamics* of a learned channel (e.g. the head mistracking a
drifting sense), and richer grounding (trade-ratio learning, social
price signals) plus a head-stability reading become the named
successors, with these numbers as motivation.

## Verdict

**ALL THREE BARS PASS at ceiling [measured, 2026-08-10, 24 seeds ×
2 arms]: the body learns its own price book, and the learned sense
carries the whole economy.**

- **LW1 — ignorance and blindness measured: PASS.** The book is
  born all-zero (naive capture peaks at worth 0.0625 — the tongue
  learning mid-lesson); the count-keyed floor cannot extract the
  trade lesson and earns **0/24** gems `[measured]`.
- **LW2 — the learned sense works: PASS at ceiling.** Worth-keyed
  on the *learned* channel: gems **24/24**, trade-led **24/24**,
  dozens per life — episode 0088's result reproduced with no
  published truth anywhere in the loop `[measured]`.
- **LW3 — the book converges: PASS, exactly.** Median learned
  prices: gem 1.000, crystal 0.100 — the α = 0.25 EMA saturates on
  ~24 taught meals per item; the demonstrations bootstrap the
  tongue exactly as they bootstrap the head `[measured]`.

What it closes: worth need not be decreed — interoceptive EMA over
felt meals is sufficient grounding at this world's stationarity, and
the whole 0088 wiring (pocket_index = the worth sense) composes with
it unchanged. What it opens (named, unlicensed): **the market** —
moving prices, other agents, meals whose pay changes — where the
EMA's stationarity assumption meets its real test, and where the
head's tracking of a *drifting* sense becomes the reading that
matters.

Reversal condition unfired (the book converged and the sense
worked).
