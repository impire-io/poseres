# Episode 0117 — The gate reshaped: references between frames, hierarchy where it earns it (2026-08-24)

Not a build or a measurement — a direction decision, recorded with
its reversal condition, hours after episode 0116 attributed the
sequence wall to the frames. The owner's call, teach-back
confirmed: the gate 0042 named "hierarchical frames" should not be
built as a hierarchy. **The mechanism to research is references
between frames** — a frame able to point at another and use it as
a constituent — with hierarchy as an emergent special case (a
chain of references where the world's structure genuinely nests),
not an imposed architecture. Flat webs, shared sub-patterns, and
nesting all fall out of one primitive instead of three.

The record already leans this way in three places [measured, prior
episodes]: recombination showed separately-taught fragments
composing 24/24 with no splice — reuse-of-parts is the kernel's
natural grain (0086); 0112's rank lesson was a reference lesson in
miniature — the win came from making the interaction between two
factors explicit, not from stacking them; and the sequence wall
itself (0115/0116) asks for a frame whose input includes another
frame's state, not a taller tower.

The price, named by the owner at the decision: **frame lifecycle
management gets harder.** Earned persistence judges each frame
alone today; references break that four ways, all on the record for
the registration that picks this up — credit assignment across
references (a load-bearing constituent may predict nothing well
alone; 0044's parked eviction-grace is adjacent machinery),
dangling references at eviction (refcount shielding risks immortal
cycles; graceful degradation is more in the house's
mortal-guesses grain, but must be measured), reference topology as
snapshot state (byte-exact, resize-surviving, draw-order-preserving
— the event-head precedent says doable), and pricing references in
the survival score (or webs bloat for free).

**The honestly open core, stated as such:** what a reference
carries — the referenced frame's pose, its prediction, a learned
binding weight, or something else — is unknown, and that is the
research, not a gap in this decision [judgment, owner's own
framing].

Reversal condition: if a reference mechanism cannot beat a plain
deeper/stacked-frame baseline on the frozen echo-teacher world
(same budget, same meters, the 0116 protocol), the simpler tower
wins on parsimony and the gate reverts to hierarchy-as-built; and
if lifecycle costs prove unpayable (eviction with references
measurably destabilizes the population where flat frames stay
healthy), the primitive is re-scoped with those numbers.
