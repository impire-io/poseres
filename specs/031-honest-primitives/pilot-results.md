## Context rows (no bars, published)

**The tax doubled**: paired improvement delta (honest − legacy) median
**−0.115**, range [−0.193, −0.054] — roughly twice the 030 macro
body's −0.043 at the same budget. Four more actions and nine more
dims cost real exploration at tiny budgets; whether the tax amortizes
over a long run (as 030's did at 2.8× budget: −0.043 → −0.009) is a
long-run question.

**The senses are alive**: hand/grid channel movement 48–75 units per
run in every seed (vs 030's 0-in-5-of-8 — `hold_next` and staging give
the drive sensed consequences to move immediately, everywhere).

**The ladder's rungs, under pure undirected exploration**:
grid actions taken 91–103 per run (8/8 seeds); a live offer appeared
in 1/8 seeds; that same seed took it at the offer moment — **planks
were crafted by chance once in 8 × 275 steps**; sticks (the deeper
rung, needing take-then-rehold-then-stage-twice) in 0/8. This is the
gradient rung-depth predicts, and it sets the **chance baseline the
long run's emergence claim must beat**: ~1 accidental planks-craft per
~2,200 undirected steps, zero accidental sticks.

## Reading

The honest body pays an upfront exploration tax and gives the brain a
ladder that chance alone climbs only to the first rung. If the
multi-week run shows planks/sticks rates well above the chance
baseline — offers *sought* rather than stumbled into — that is
emergence, and it is exactly what the reversal condition's opposite
looks like. All numbers above go to the journey episode either way.
