# Episode 0096 — The lab wearing reality's skin: c1e closes, and the scenario is the verdict (2026-08-11)

The ladder's top rung ran, in one day: two attempts, four runs, five
open amendments, a two-arm design from the owner (lives versus
provisioning), and a close that answered a bigger question than the one
registered.

**What the run measured** (full readings in the run plan's closing
section; every amendment carries its raw numbers):

- Reality taxes everything [measured]: digs are client-side wall-clock
  (~3 s at any world speed); drops far-scatter past pickup at 5× and
  despawn in 60 s wall; pose is spawn-anchor-relative; the wealth sense
  saturates at 64 items; the bot built a pedestal in its own bed cell.
  Each fault was found by exact corpse-line arithmetic, probed on the
  live world, amended openly, and re-run.
- The composition's parts all work in reality [measured]: the hold,
  once aimed by the body's own senses instead of a hand-computed goal,
  held at 98–100% dwell every segment of every arm; the head's
  prediction error stayed at lab levels; taught skills expressed.
- Neither life design survived [measured]: the provisioned one-shot
  died the **pantry death** at 14,398 — crafting its stockpile paid
  like work at a third of the cost, so it ate its seed corn and starved
  at the grove; the respawn arm cycled 28 deaths in 140k steps with
  first-income per life NEVER trending down — deaths cleared the
  crafting stock while online learning eroded the taught skill (the
  churn of 0091–0094, measured in the wild). The frozen decision rule
  returned both post-mortems to design.

**The verdict that closes it** [judgment, the owner's, seconded by the
record]: the scenario was the tangent. We ran a synthetic metabolism —
hand-dosed, five-times-amended — inside a world whose native survival
economy (hunger, food, health, death, respawn) we had switched off; we
pinned the bot to one spot in a world with nothing else in it and
logged exploration as a disease, in the project whose richest Minecraft
behavior ever recorded (c1c) was exploration. Crafting was meant to be
the probe for "can it live" and became the definition of living. The
architecture was never the tangent — everything shipped is
embodiment-general and came out of this run sharper (the goal is now
captured from the taught senses; the meter pays on the world's record).
The life we asked it to live was the lab wearing reality's skin.

What it opens: the native-survival reframe — the world's own meter
(the vitals channels the body has carried unused since feature 027),
real food as pay, native death and respawn as the game frame,
exploration as foraging — the owner's call to register.

Reversal condition: the scenario verdict reverses if a
synthetic-meter life in a barren world is ever shown reaching the
registered goal (2,000 chains past 1M steps) under these shipped
components — that would prove the scenario adequate and relocate the
failure into the composition.
