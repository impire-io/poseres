# Value economy — does survival pressure re-price the menu?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

The menu gate (episode 0080) measured *what turns the menu*: novelty.
The meter gates (G4/G4b) measured *that stakes work*: the composition
feeds itself when every gain pays the same +0.1. This topic joins
them and makes the payments unequal — a lean economy where the chain
products nourish and quick cobble is junk food — and asks the owner's
"survival of the fittest" question in registrable form: does survival
pressure alone, with no praise and no new wiring, shift *choice*
toward what nourishes? The honest mechanism prediction is recorded
now: **no repricing** — the drive values novelty of endings and has
no calorie sense; energy is sensed but nothing links it to a
terminal's worth `[mechanism-argument]`. If the measurement agrees,
the missing layer (a deficit→value coupling: hunger must reach
wanting) is named with numbers behind it; if it refutes the
prediction, something in the composition already prices survival —
either answer shapes the next design.

## The question

Under a meter whose per-product payments differ 40× (the lean table),
does the ten-recipe menu's choice distribution shift toward
nourishing recipes relative to a flat-payment control — and does the
menu-fed life outlive a junk-commanded one?

## Instrument (frozen with the bars)

- **Cohort**: fresh 24 seeds at **34 dims** — body 32 + verdict
  (position-gated judge on R5, unchanged) + **energy** (a meter
  channel present from birth, the G4 precedent). Teaching identical
  to the menu cohort otherwise: 45 wood segments + 2 passes over the
  same ten tapes, EP 26, `event_head_eta = 0.5`.
- **The lean table** (per gain-*tick*, the meter's measured
  semantics): stick **+0.15**, oak_planks **+0.04**, oak_log
  **+0.04**, cobblestone **+0.004**. Drain 0.0005/tick with the G4b
  taper (ramp 1,500 → full at 3,000). Flat control pays +0.1 for any
  gain (G4's measured dose). The pilot may recalibrate the table
  openly (raw numbers in JOURNEY, before any 24-seed arm) if it
  proves degenerate — all-live or all-die in both pilot arms.
- **Arms** (24 seeds, H = 5,000): **V0** flat meter + menu (β 0);
  **V1** lean meter + menu (β 0); **V2** lean meter + praise on
  cobble (β 0.5 — episode 0080's measured command, used here as the
  junk-diet forcing instrument).
- **Policy**: the shipped `RecipePolicy` exactly as in the menu gate
  (label channel 32); selection trace, attribution, and
  classification rules identical to episode 0080's.
- **Pilot before the arms** (seeds 1–8, V1 + V2 only, published):
  verifies the table separates the diets and the 34-dim cohort's
  composition works under the lean drain.

## Pre-registered bars

- **Bar E1 — the meter distinguishes diets:** V1 alive at H for
  **≥ 18/24** seeds AND V1's median final energy exceeds V2's by
  **≥ 0.2**. (Robust whether V2 dies or merely runs lean; if V2
  survives on side-chains mined en route, the leak is published —
  that is a finding about the obsession, not a bar failure.)
- **Bar E2 — the repricing read (two named outcomes, both
  decisive):** nourishing share (R8+R9+R10 attributed completions /
  total) in V1 is **≥ 1.5×** its share in V0 → repricing is real and
  the registered prediction is refuted. Below 1.5× → **no
  repricing**: the drive cannot smell calories, and the
  deficit→value coupling becomes the named successor with these
  numbers as its motivation.
- **Bar E3 — work under stakes:** V1 chains **≥ 18/24** (the
  composition keeps working while the economy is lean).

Registered readings: death ticks and final-energy spreads per arm;
choice distributions V0 vs V1 (the repricing curve per recipe);
energy-channel value traces (does low energy *attract* the
novelty drive — the perverse case — or repel it?).

## Reversal condition

This topic assumes recipe choice is where survival pressure would
show. If V1 seeds die ≥ 7/24 *while still rotating normally* (choice
unchanged, work intact, income insufficient), the lean table is
mispriced rather than the question answered — recalibrate openly and
rerun before any verdict. And if E2's share shifts WITHOUT the meter
(i.e., V0 drifts equally from episode 0080's flat-meter anchor), the
34-dim cohort — not the economy — moved the choice; the comparison
is V1-vs-V0 within-cohort for exactly this reason.
