**Two amendments, with the raw numbers above:**

1. **The economy was degenerate**: econ3 tripled collect time without
   touching drain — perfect work earns ~0.0028/tick against drain 0.005,
   so competence-as-registered (income ≥ drain) is PHYSICALLY impossible
   there; the taught seeds' 192–244 gains in 20k confirm it. P3's economy
   is recalibrated so a competent adult clears break-even with the same
   margin ratio the measured economy has (drain scaled to the 3×-taxed
   achievable income) — the tax stays real (3× slower), survival stays
   possible.
2. **The rule gains the parent's patience as a purse, not a date**: full
   shortfall coverage draws down a finite purse; demonstrated income
   extends it (a matching grant). Competent children wean as before;
   incompetent ones exhaust the purse and the world decides. Purse and
   match constants are declared in units of drain-coverage time and
   explored at pilot; the confirmatory freezes one rule.
