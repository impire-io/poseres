# Whose envelope? — the drive's, or the frames'

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-24

## Abstract

Sequence-encoding (episode 0115) ended on a measured wall that was
nobody's registered suspect: the competence envelope. Every declared
sense served inside it; beyond it, behavior plateaued
budget-invariantly (0.53–0.68 violations/step against random's
exact 0.750) regardless of encoding capacity, and every meter died
with the behavior. Design 0020 names the discriminating
registration this topic runs: the plateau's two candidate owners
make opposite predictions on the same frozen world. If the envelope
is the **drive's** — one-step curiosity has no structural reason to
finish long sequences — then the shipped completion/commitment
stack (designs 0014/0016, built for exactly this shape: incumbency
while progress advances, finishing what is started) should move the
wall. If the envelope is the **frames'** — capacity for deep
sequential state — the stack changes nothing, and hierarchical
frames (0042's named gate) arrive with numbers already in hand.
Either verdict completes the language road's map: the teacher-world
runs with the stack, or waits on frames.

## The question

On the frozen echo-teacher world, does enabling the shipped
completion/commitment stack (the itch on the world's own progress
channel, commitment, event head at the shipped η) move the
sequence-competence plateau that the plain drive could not — or
does the wall stand, attributing the envelope to the frames?

## Pre-registered bars

Protocol inherited frozen from 0115: the echo-teacher world v1
exactly (m=4, R/C families, T=2P acceptance, reset-on-violation,
progress + pulse as the teacher's whole voice), 13,000-step budget,
24 seeds, the frozen acceptance rule (median ≥ max(3× measured
random floor, 2.0/1k back half)) and the conformity meter
(violations/step; floor 0.750 exact). Encodings fixed at 0115's
measured leaders — **W2 on the R family, DK on the C family** — so
the policy stack is the ONE variable. The plain-drive baselines are
0115's frozen rows, inherited on the record (same world, same
budget, same seeds). The stack arm: `CompletionItchPolicy` with the
shipped operating points (κ = 0.25, commit_kappa = 0.1,
explore_defers_holds on, event head η = 0.5), progress_index = the
world's own progress channel — the exact mapping journaled at rig
time, before any arm runs.

- **Bar E0 — parity inside the envelope:** the stack arm on R(2)/W2
  and C(2)/DK passes the frozen acceptance rule and lands within
  1.25× of the 0115 plain arm's violations/step on the same rungs —
  the stack must not break what already works. If E0 fails at the
  shipped dose, the κ dose may be amended openly (Doc 0011's
  protocol) before any E1 claim; if no dose passes E0, the
  completion machinery does not map to this world's progress
  semantics — a design finding, journaled with the numbers.
- **Bar E1 — the envelope moves (headline):** on R(4)/W2, the stack
  arm passes the frozen acceptance rule (≥ 2.0/1k median, where the
  plain drive read 0.462) AND its violations/step median improves
  on the plain plateau by ≥ 0.05 (0.601 → ≤ 0.551). PASS attributes
  the envelope (at least partly) to the drive; the teacher-world
  proceeds with the stack.
- **Bar E2 — the reach of the move:** the stack arm on R(8)/W2 and
  C(4)/DK, read against the same frozen rule — the new edge of the
  envelope, measured. E2 is informative: its FAIL after an E1 PASS
  narrows the moved envelope without refuting the attribution.

## Reversal condition

- **E1 fails with E0 passing** (the stack costs nothing and buys
  nothing): drive-alignment is refuted at the shipped dose and
  mapping — the envelope is attributed to the frames, hierarchical
  frames become 0042's gate with numbers, and the topic graduates
  to design updating 0020 with the attribution.
- **E0 fails at every openly-amended dose:** the completion stack's
  progress semantics do not transfer to sequence worlds — itself a
  design 0016 boundary finding; routing to design with the mapping
  lesson.
- Standing guards inherited (0115): thresholds frozen before arms;
  no world revision post-freeze; instrument readings are trail; the
  0115 baselines are frozen inheritance, never re-run selectively.

## Verdict

Measuring complete 2026-08-24; the discriminating design answered.

- **Bar E0 — PASS at stack-nc, via the declared dose ladder**
  [measured]: the shipped dose failed E0 (C2 slashed 3.6×, false
  completions 35–52%); the ablation separated the components —
  commitment OFF, itch full: R2 25.846 (+32% over plain), C2
  23.385 (plain's level), clauses cleared. Incumbency, not the
  itch, taxed the token switch [measured by ablation]; a
  boundary-releasing hold is the sequence-commitment shape, named
  for design 0020.
- **Bar E1 — FAIL as registered** [measured]: R(4) acceptance
  0.769 vs the frozen 2.0 line (plain 0.462; 22/24 accepting, up
  from 16); the conformity clause met (−0.118). The wall moved
  interior meters and did not fall.
- **Bar E2 — the moved interior** [measured]: R(8) violations
  0.458 (plain 0.627) with 0/24 accepting; C(4) 0.615 with 23/24
  touching. Conformity −0.10 to −0.17 at every edge rung.

Load-bearing claims: **the envelope is shared, and the frames hold
the wall** — the drive's misalignment was real and fixable (itch
without incumbency beats plain inside the envelope, moves every
interior meter beyond it), and with it removed the wall stands: the
residual is representational [measured — the discriminating
design's intended read]; **the itch is a free win for sequence
worlds** (+32% inside the envelope, licensed for the teacher-world's
bounded-context configuration) [measured]; **incumbency taxes
switches** — 0101's lesson runs backward in sequence worlds
[measured by ablation]; **0042's plateau prediction is confirmed at
its named location** — hierarchical frames is now the measured
gate [measured chain: 0112 actions, 0114 exceptions, 0115
encoding, this topic's attribution]. Confounds as journaled: one
world; the frozen 2.0 line inherits 0115's meter (partial
competence reads conservatively); dose ladder limited to the two
declared configurations.
