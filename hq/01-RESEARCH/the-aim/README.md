# The aim — can the palate steer the hungry body toward seen food?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-15

## Abstract

Three readings triangulate on one missing arrow. The head reading found
the dig learned true but the body parked (nothing predicts gain); D3's
reversal found that hunger must reach the START of the chain; the F
campaign found the flood initiates foraging and reliably finishes the
meal — but FIRST CONTACT is still spatial luck: stalling lives roam the
taught lanes where no melons live, and the flood cannot steer. This
topic couples three proven mechanisms — the distal senses (D1, live),
the learned palate (0089, worth eaten into existence), and the flood
(intrusion form, measured) — into the missing arrow: seen signature ×
learned worth × current deficit → approach. A pass gives the hungry
body its first aimed contact, connects the drops leg (dig → see drop →
walk → pickup) that the live world's delayed pay starved, and finally
gives R-explore its real reading; a fail says aim is not the missing
layer and re-opens the motivation-stack ordering.

## The question

Released hungry into a world whose food it can see, does the taught
composition first-contact food by worth-keyed aim — the palate's
learned prices applied to the distal senses under the flood — where the
same body with worth decoupled still finds food only by luck?
