# Episode 0089 — The tongue learns: worth without decree (2026-08-10)

Episode 0088 gave the brain eyes for trade by pointing its wealth
sense at a worth channel — but the world published the prices. The
learned-worth topic removed the decree: the body is born with an
all-zero price book and learns each item's worth from its own felt
meals (an EMA toward the meter's pay, α = 0.25), the book carried
across lessons as body state — the world resets between lessons, the
tongue does not. Pay *is* the metabolic truth in this world, so
worth is grounded in feeding, not authority; the demonstrations
bootstrap the palate exactly as they bootstrap the head (the taught
trade is the body's first taste of a gem).

All three bars passed at ceiling [measured]:

- **Born blind, measurably**: with the naive book the worth channel
  barely whispers (peak 0.0625 during a full trade lesson), and the
  count-keyed floor stays exactly as blind as episode 0088 measured
  — the lesson unextractable, **0/24** ever holding a gem.
- **The learned sense carries everything**: worth-keyed on the
  learned channel, **24/24** seeds trade (trade-led 24/24, dozens of
  gems per life), composing the never-demonstrated collect-then-
  trade journey — 0088's ceiling reproduced with no published truth
  anywhere in the loop.
- **The book converges exactly**: median learned prices gem 1.000,
  crystal 0.100 — two dozen taught meals saturate the palate.

The chain of the last three episodes, plainly: 0086 showed lessons
compose through the world; 0088 showed wealth is a *sense*, not a
count; 0089 shows that sense need not be given — it can be **eaten
into existence**. Teachers demonstrate, the body tastes, the palate
prices, the machinery trades.

What it opens (named, unlicensed): **the market** — moving prices,
other agents, meals whose pay drifts. There the EMA's stationarity
assumption meets its real test, and the head's tracking of a
drifting sense becomes the load-bearing reading. That is also where
c1e's real Minecraft world naturally leads.

Reversal condition: none — records a completed measurement. A
non-stationary economy where the converged-book condition holds but
trading fails reopens the question at the market successor, not
here.
