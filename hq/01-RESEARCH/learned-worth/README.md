# Learned worth — can the body learn its own price book?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

Episode 0088 restored trade sight by pointing `pocket_index` at a
worth channel — but the world *published* the prices. A brain headed
for markets must learn what things are worth from experience. The
minimal honest grounding is already in the stack: **worth is what
feeds you**. The body keeps a per-item price book, updated
interoceptively from its own felt meals (the meter's pay at each
gain), and publishes the worth channel as Σ count · *learned* price
— born ignorant, converging toward the world's metabolic truth as
the body eats. The teachers matter once more: the demonstrations are
where the body first *tastes* each item (the taught trade is the
first gem meal), so lessons bootstrap the price book exactly as they
bootstrap the head. The gate asks whether 0088's result survives
the move from published truth to learned estimate.

## The question

With prices learned (per-item EMA of felt pay, α = 0.25; the book is
body state carried across lessons — the world resets, the tongue
does not) and never published, does the worth-keyed machinery still
learn, elect, and compose the trade — and does the book converge to
the metabolic truth?

  all-zero (instrument assertion) AND the count-keyed floor stays
  blind: lesson B unextractable, gems ≤ **2/24**.

## Reversal condition

If LW2 fails while the book is verified converged (LW3's condition
met on the taught cohort) and extraction verified, then felt-EMA
grounding is insufficient even with a correct book — the failure is
in the *dynamics* of a learned channel (e.g. the head mistracking a
drifting sense), and richer grounding (trade-ratio learning, social
price signals) plus a head-stability reading become the named
successors, with these numbers as motivation.
