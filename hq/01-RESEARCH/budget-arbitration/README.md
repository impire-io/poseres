# Budget arbitration — can the itch and hold gains scale themselves?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-11

## Abstract

The completion itch's κ and the hold's λ are fixed gains, hand-tuned into
the drive's measured terminal-value band (Doc 0011's protocol). They work —
but they are OUR numbers, and the tuning protocol itself proves they are
scale-relative: what matters is where they sit against the drive's own
value distribution. This topic asks whether the arbitration can normalize
itself against the brain's running drive statistics — the motivation-stack
charter's budget layer, entered at its cheapest gate. A pass retires two
dials into one dimensionless setting; a clean fail records that arbitration
scale is genuinely structural.

## The question

Can self-scaled arbitration (gains normalized by running drive-value
statistics, one dimensionless setting) match the hand-tuned gains on the
measured instruments, and keep working in a world whose value scale shifts
fivefold — where fixed gains demonstrably mis-arbitrate?

## Pre-registered bars

- **Bar B1 (parity):** on the recorded instruments (sample-field taught
  cohort; C1 lab taught cohort), self-scaled arbitration lands within 2
  seeds of the recorded hand-tuned rows on their headline counts (eating /
  chains), 24 seeds each, no hand κ or λ anywhere.
- **Bar B2 (transfer — the point):** on a ×5 value-scale variant world
  (felt-pay magnitudes rescaled, nothing else), the hand-tuned arm degrades
  by ≥ 6 seeds from its B1 level while the self-scaled arm stays within 2
  of its own. If the hand gains do NOT degrade, the premise collapses —
  fixed κ is already scale-free; record it and abandon.
- **Bar B3 (no pathology):** the perseveration watch stays clean (no
  target-agnostic digging in the hundreds), and the moving-senses ruin rate
  is not worsened against its recorded linear anchor.

## Reversal condition

If self-scaling needs per-world clamps or floors to pass B2, the dial has
relocated rather than retired — park the topic with the measured clamp
values and return the budget-layer question to the owner.

## Verdict

- **Bar B1 — PASS** [measured]: self-scaled arbitration (k̂ = l̂ = 4.142,
  calibrated once; scale = EMA of the witnessed per-step drive-value
  spread) reaches parity on the sample-field cohort — 8/8 alive, refined
  median 159 vs hand 160, gains median 466 vs 467; κ_eff self-lands at
  0.19–0.21 against the hand 0.25.
- **Bar B2 — PREMISE COLLAPSED** [measured]: under the registered ×5
  felt-pay rescale the hand gains do NOT degrade (8/8 alive, refined
  median 124 vs 159 — a dip, zero failures), because the witnessed drive
  spread moved only ~1.4× under the 5× world-side push: **the drive
  pipeline is already largely scale-invariant**. The bar's own escape
  fires: κ is not the per-world magic number the premise assumed — which
  is why the same 0.25 has worked across every body in the record
  [mechanism-argument, corroborated by Doc 0011's cross-body table].
- **Bar B3 — not reached** (moot with B2 collapsed; no pathology seen in
  any pilot arm).

Abandoned as registered. The finding stands: the dial's portability is a
property of the drive normalization, not of luck — self-scaling works but
solves a problem the composition does not have.
