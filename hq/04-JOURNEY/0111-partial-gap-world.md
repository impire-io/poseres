# Episode 0111 — Partial-gap world: supply cannot outrun a camper (2026-08-18)

Design 0017 left one gate in front of every lean-worlds follow-up: a
world whose blind-brain hostile reading is a *partial* gap (below-12
steady-state in 0.10–0.90), where 0110's larder-size axis produced
only 0.000 or 1.000. This topic ran the design's cheapest candidate
axis the day after graduation: renewal rate. The 0110 rig, restored
onto its own world (`pgw1-minecraft`), larder frozen at C-1's
total-war shape (one patch, 2 pre-grown melons) plus 2 age-7 stems
whose fruit slots the pre-grown melons block — regrowth begins only
when a melon is consumed — and ONE walked variable, the world's
`random_tick_speed`, ladder T12→T0 declared before any rung ran.

The instrument taught first [measured]: the playerless I0 read **0
growth events at every speed**, and the diagnosis (stems verified in
place; wheat frozen at age 0 for 60 s under tick speed 255 in a
forceloaded chunk; the same wheat racing to age 7 the moment one
idle player joined) pinned a server fact — 1.21.11 random-ticks only
chunks near an online player; forceload loads without ticking. The
second edition (idle peer as the ticking presence, amended openly
before any rung) priced the rungs: 57.5/32.7/12.5/7.5 melons per
life-equivalent at T12/T6/T3/T1, linear in tick speed — even T1's
ceiling covers a solo body's 3-eat minimum, and solo arms confirmed
it (below-12 0.000 everywhere, eats 6/5/7/7, zero starvation; Bar
P0 PASS at every rung).

Then the walk refuted its own registered expectation at the top rung
and never stopped [measured]: hostile-86 below-12 steady-state
**1.000 at T12, T6, T3, and T1 alike** — zero eats, zero collects,
in every arm, at every renewal rate (Bar P1 FAIL: the band is
empty; Bar P2 never reached). The act logs behind the claim: the
peer dug 21/7/13/10 melons per arm, alive and on the patch square
to the end. The mechanism, measured across the whole axis: the
hostile peer shadows the subject, the subject forages the one
patch, so renewal lands exactly where the peer stands; standing
stock never exceeds the two fruit slots, and every unit of supply
is contested at script speed the moment it exists. **Supply rate
cannot open a middle against a camper.** The registered reversal
fired as written — the all-or-nothing verdict generalizes from
larder size to renewal rate — and the topic graduates abandoned
carrying the second null on design 0017's axis 2.

What it taught / what it opened: a partial gap needs the rival to
be *somewhere else some of the time* — spatially separated patches,
where WHERE the rival stands decides what is contested, are now
axis 2's remaining arm, and comparable-speed bodies (axis 3) its
successor [judgment — design 0017's candidate list, measured down
by one]. The rig legacy: the renewal dial (stems + tick speed, with
consumption-triggered regrowth by construction), the I0
harvest-poll instrument, and the server-tick lesson (expectations
from the world's measured facts — the 0110 L2 lesson, now one rung
deeper: the *server* is part of the world).

Reversal condition: a measured hostile-86 below-12 steady-state in
0.10–0.90 on any declared world — a separated-patch geometry, a
non-shadowing rival policy, or a comparable-speed second brain —
reopens the partial-gap hunt on that world, and design 0017's
definition of done resumes from its item 2.
