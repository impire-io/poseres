# Episode 0110 — Lean worlds: race beats information (2026-08-17)

Episode 0109 closed with a reopening condition — meters paying on a
non-renewable food world — and the evidence arrived the next day, on
purpose: lean-worlds ran the successor premise. Calibrate the leanest
solo-survivable world, send in the adversary, and ask whether an
attribution sense earns its license through *outcomes* this time,
prediction error having been the wrong premise once already.

What measured true [measured]: the calibration walk taught a meter
fact first — at this life length (15,075 steps) solo hunger is
unreachable on ANY declared rung; even two non-renewing melons coast
at steady-state below-12 = 0.000, so the entire question rides on
the delta a second body causes (L0; C-1 frozen at the exact
3-eat minimum). Then the premise finally bit: on two melons the 0109
adversary drove the subject to below-12 = **1.000** vs solo 0.000 —
zero eats, zero collects, food pinned at 5–7, the peer's whole act
log **2 digs** in the birth minute (L1 PASS, the first measured
co-habitation cost in two topics). The head heard nothing wrong
except hunger itself: ex-flood, the emptied world was again *easier*
to predict while the body starved in it — 0109's dissociation
completed in both directions.

The license was honored in the house grammar: the peers sense
(nearest other body — presence, egocentric bearing, distance, count,
sha256 signature of the world's own username) landed opt-in at the
bridge (`PEERS=1`) and anatomy (`c1_anatomy(peers=True)`, 94/13,
appended last, default untouched, contract-checked live). Bar L2
passed **27/27** on the *second edition* of its reading — the first
edition read 9/16 and the instrument caught its own assumption: the
subject stood 2.3 blocks from where tp claimed, the solved
displacement matching the channel to three decimals, so the reading
became self-locating (expectations from the world's own measured
facts, never from commanded teleports; the D1 lesson, one rung
deeper).

Then the sense met the war and bought nothing [measured]. A fresh
45/45 teach (no peer in any classroom — the channel read zeros
through every lesson), a passing solo baseline (0.0912, with its
expression-latency wobble journaled: the new draw blanked its first
segment and destroyed half the larder learning), and hostile arms at
every scarcity rung: 2 melons — total loss, identical to the blind
brain, the registered slack guard firing; 4 melons — no gap for
anyone (the blind brain ate MORE than the sensing one); 3 melons
(Amendment 2, registered before it ran) — no gap either. The flip
from everything to nothing happens entirely between 2 and 3 melons.
**Contests over fast, non-renewable resources are all-or-nothing —
decided by race, not information** [measured, this peer speed and
geometry]. The peers channel was alive and learning in every hostile
arm (err 0.0036–0.0065, falling) and never touched an outcome. L3
FAILED per Amendment 2's pre-registered routing.

What was refuted or reversed: the last premise standing from the
original contested-worlds ladder — that an attribution sense, made
instrument-grade and given a world where contention demonstrably
bites, would pay in outcomes. It could not, because no tested world
offered a middle: below the knife edge behavior has no slack, above
it there is no war. The L3-after-L1 reversal fired exactly as
registered, and per the senses-first rule this is the one licensed
moment for machinery: the topic graduates to design
[0017](../02-DESIGN/0017-lean-worlds.md) carrying the question "what
makes information about another body worth anything?" — with
teaching (no brain has ever been TAUGHT about peers), world geometry
(regrowth slower than joint consumption; the unexplored middle), and
comparable-speed bodies as the measured-down candidate axes.

What it taught / what it opened: the meters were the right premise
and the world was the wrong shape; a sense pays only where behavior
has slack; expression latency decides birth-minute wars (the two
teach draws differed by one segment of latency and it decided every
race); and the two-topic arc leaves a durable instrument estate —
the peers sense (opt-in, proven), its self-locating reading (ported
to `examples/minecraft/survival/arms/peers_reading.py`), the
two-body rig with three peer modes, and the parametric lean-world
builder, all in the trail.

Reversal condition: if a future reading shows a world with a
*partial* blind-brain gap (hostile-86 below-12 steady-state in
0.10–0.90, any geometry), the design's axes become testable and
0017's first registered topic runs on that world; and if a
peers-taught brain (axis 1) closes ≥ 50% of such a gap, the sense's
outcome license is granted after all.
