## The mechanism, final form (opt-in, promotion pending)

1. `commit_kappa` (0.1 — above the largest L1-measured flip margin):
   repeating the previous action while sensed progress advances earns
   incumbency — hysteresis against frame-noise on a knife-edge vote.
2. `explore_defers_holds`: the ε-gate defers while a hold advances.
3. The intention boundary: a progress collapse (the world's own
   completion/reset) clears the incumbent — incumbency dies with its
   intention; without it the first committed arm locked DIG for 517
   consecutive frames (perseveration, the twin).

Defaults 0.0/False are bit-exact shipped behavior (L3: full gate
green, byte-frozen suite included). Promotion into the shipped
default is a spec-kit feature decision, not this topic's.
