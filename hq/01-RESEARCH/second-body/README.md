# Second body — is the motivation stack general, or Minecraft-shaped?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

Every measurement in the goals arc (episodes 0069–0083) ran on one
embodiment: the C1 Minecraft anatomy and its lab world. The shipped
mechanisms are body-agnostic *by construction* — channel indices are
constructor parameters, the head predicts whatever channels exist —
but body-agnostic *by measurement* is a different claim, and only
measured closes a debate. The owner named the risk plainly ("we need
to make sure we are not type-casting ourselves into only a game bot
AI"). This gate mounts the **identical shipped composition** — event
head, completion itch, brain-side hold, recipe memory and policy,
the felt-meal coupling instrument — on a second, deliberately
alien body, with **zero changes to `src/pra`**, and asks whether the
measured behaviors reappear.

## The question

Does the composition transfer to a structurally different
embodiment — election, taught order, and the emergency appetite —
with nothing changed but the constructor indices and the teaching
tapes?

## Instrument (frozen with the bars)

- **The sample-field body** (harness-built, the anatomy layer's
  job): a rover on a bounded field, structurally different from the
  C1 body on every axis the composition might secretly lean on —
  **absolute compass moves, no facing/turning** (the C1 motor space
  is yaw-centric); **7 actions** (vs 12); **position channels not at
  indices (0,1) and not on a /64 scale**; a ~14-channel body (vs
  32) plus felt + energy appended. Acquisition is a held-intention
  **collect** with sensed progress (6 ticks, vs C1's 12/3), two
  materials: **ore** (one step, junk pay) and **crystal**, which a
  **refine** action converts to **refined** (the two-step chain
  analog, nourishing pay). Lean table: refined +0.15, crystal
  +0.04, ore +0.004; felt = clip(pay/0.15, 0, 1); drain and taper
  as episode 0083 (0.0015, ramp 1,500→3,000), pilot-calibratable
  openly.
- **The brain and policies are the shipped classes, untouched**:
  `CompletionItchPolicy`/`RecipePolicy` mounted with the
  sample-field's indices; the deficit-gating instrument fork
  identical to episode 0083's six lines. General vocabulary
  throughout (acquisition channel, felt channel, meter) — the
  language-audit concern applies to this registration itself.
- **Teaching**: a fresh 24-seed cohort taught by demonstration
  tapes, mirroring the arc's protocol — 45 chain segments (collect
  crystal → refine) then two passes over **seven** station
  demonstrations (three crystal fields, three ore patches, the full
  chain), hungry teaching (segments start at energy 0.5) so every
  recipe's terminal carries its own taste through the unchanged
  extraction.
- **Arms** (24 seeds, H = 5,000): **T0** no-itch floor (frames'
  drive alone — the G1 shape); **T1** the composition (head + itch
  + recipe hold, label off); **T2** the deficit-gated coupling
  (κ_h = 0.1). Pilot (seeds 1–8, T1 + T2) before any 24-seed arm:
  instrument sanity (senses honest, head learning — prediction
  error falling), drain calibration; amendments only openly with
  raw numbers.

## Reversal condition

This gate assumes the composition's generality. If **S1 fails**
while the instrument is verified honest (senses correct, the head's
prediction error falling on the new body), the composition is
measured Minecraft-shaped: the "general brain" language in the
public story must be qualified until the specific dependency is
isolated (a named follow-up per failing layer), and c1e proceeds as
a game result, not a generality result.

## Verdict

**ALL THREE BARS PASS [measured, 2026-08-10, 24 seeds × 3 arms] —
the motivation stack is general, not Minecraft-shaped.** Identical
shipped classes, an alien body (absolute moves, no facing, 7
actions, 16 channels, different indices and scales), zero changes
to `src/pra`.

- **S1 — election transfers: PASS 24/24** refined seeds (floor
  2/24 — the drive alone barely ever completes the chain, the arc's
  every floor reproduced on body #2) `[measured]`.
- **S2 — taught order transfers: PASS 24/24** seeds acquiring at a
  taught far station `[measured]`.
- **S3′ (as amended openly pre-arms) — the appetite transfers:
  PASS** — hungry-bin nourishing share 0.996 vs the uncoupled
  0.780 (margin +0.216 ≥ 0.15); deaths **0/24 vs 16/24** (≤ half);
  dial monotone 0.870 → 0.967 → 0.996 `[measured]`. The printed
  runner line evaluates the superseded ratio form and reads FAIL;
  the amended bar's arithmetic is stated here and in JOURNEY — the
  amendment predates the arms, with the pilot's raw numbers.
- **The survival ladder on body #2**: drive alone 24/24 dead
  (G4's frontier-starvation reproduced on a second embodiment);
  uncoupled composition 16/24 dead at the biting drain; coupled
  composition **0/24 dead** `[measured]`. On this body the
  emergency appetite is not +4 lives — it is the difference between
  mostly dying and nobody dying.

Honest gap recorded: the registered head-prediction-error reading
was not instrumented in the rows; the head's learning of the alien
body is evidenced behaviorally (the hold's far reaches and the
itch's elections both require it) `[mechanism-argument]` — a future
body-port should log the curve directly.

Reversal condition unfired. The type-casting concern is answered
with numbers: the stack's claims are embodiment-general as
measured on two structurally disjoint bodies.
