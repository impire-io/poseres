# Episode 0116 — Whose envelope? Shared — and the frames hold the wall (2026-08-24)

The language road's last open question, run as design 0020's
discriminating registration the day after it was named: is the
sequence-competence envelope the drive's (nothing makes one-step
curiosity finish long sequences) or the frames' (no capacity for
deep sequential state)? The two owners make opposite predictions on
the same frozen world, so the topic enabled the shipped
completion/commitment stack — the machinery built for finishing
what is started — against 0115's inherited plain-drive baselines,
encodings pinned at the measured leaders, the policy stack the one
variable, the anatomy mapping journaled before any arm (the itch on
the world's own progress channel; a completion fires on a predicted
acceptance).

What the arms taught, in order: the shipped dose FAILED parity —
conformity improved at every rung (growing with difficulty) but
C(2) acceptance was slashed 3.6× with false completions peaking
where token switches live [measured]. The registered dose ladder
separated the components by ablation [measured]: with commitment
OFF and the itch full, acceptance rose ABOVE the plain drive inside
the envelope (R(2) 25.846 vs 19.615, +32%; C(2) back at plain's
level) — **incumbency, not the itch, taxed the switch**. The 0101
lesson runs backward in sequence worlds: what the dig needed —
dying with its intention — the token switch punishes; a
boundary-releasing hold is the sequence-commitment shape, named in
design 0020. Bar E0 PASS at the ablated configuration; Bar E1 then
read the wall itself: R(4) acceptance rose 1.7× (0.769, 22/24 seeds
accepting) with the conformity clause met (−0.118) — **and the
frozen 2.0 line stood unmet: FAIL as registered.** E2 measured the
moved interior: R(8) conformity 0.458 (plain 0.627) with not one
full sequence; C(4) 23/24 touching.

The attribution the design was built to yield [measured]: **the
envelope is shared, and the frames hold the wall.** The drive's
misalignment was real and fixable — repaired, it beats plain
everywhere inside the envelope and moves every interior meter
beyond it — and with it repaired the wall stands. What remains when
the brain finally wants to finish and still cannot is
representational: the frames do not carry deep sequential state.
Episode 0042's pre-registered prediction — "the current kernel
plateaus short of syntax, because nothing in it composes;
hierarchical frames become the next named gate" — receives its full
measured confirmation at its named location, closing a
prediction-to-evidence arc thirteen months of episodes wide: 0112
(actions), 0114 (exceptions), 0115 (encoding), 0116 (the
attribution).

What it opened: **hierarchical frames is now the measured gate**,
not the predicted one — kernel research, the vision's named next
mountain; and the **itch without incumbency is a free win** for
sequence worlds (+32% inside the envelope), licensed for a
bounded-context teacher-world configuration that could run today
within the measured envelope. Design
[0020](../02-DESIGN/0020-sequence-encoding.md) carries both.

Reversal condition: a hierarchical-frames (or other kernel)
mechanism that clears R(4)'s frozen line on this world overturns
the wall's attribution to representation-as-built and re-opens the
envelope ladders; a boundary-releasing hold that beats the ablated
itch re-licenses commitment for sequence worlds with its numbers.
