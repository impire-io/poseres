# Bars D2/D3 — the seen forage and the gate differential (2026-08-13)

**Verdicts: D2 SPLIT (chains 0/5 FAIL, completions PASS); D3 FAIL.**
The registered reversal fires as written: the senses are demonstrably
live and D2 still failed — seeing was necessary but not sufficient,
and the remaining gap is machinery, which needs its own registration
(the design conversation, not this topic).

## The runs

Teaching: 45/45 sense-using lessons (three interleaved classroom
variants — adjacent, walk-to-seen, turn-to-seen; eat-heavy tails of
3–5 chained consumes on the game-tick chew), zero retries after one
geometry fix (facing south, the body's right is WEST — the
left-handed frame again; V2's stand moved east of its melon).
D2: one hungry-born 25,125-step life, gate on (`d2-status.jsonl`).
D3: three paired hungry-born 6,000-step lives per arm, gate on vs
off (`d3-rows.json`; a first attempt with 3,000-step lives — shorter
than the measured expression latency — was discarded as instrument
miscalibration and is recorded here).

## What the senses bought, measured

Before them (the 33-dim keyhole body): zero eats in every life ever
run — 20k arm steps plus all probe lives. With them: **2 of 7
hungry-born lives expressed the full forage chain** — D2's life ate 5
times (first at step 3,275, one distant collect feeding the chain of
eats), and D3's ungated life 2 foraged completely (6 eats, first at
1,491, one full seen-forage CHAIN: distant collect → eat). And the
completion pathway is alive for the first time: **446 completions
fired, 256 realized** in the D2 life against a lifetime total of zero
before the drops sense existed. The eyes work, the mouth works, the
bell rings.

## Why the bars still failed

- **D2 chains 0/5**: after its one successful forage-and-feast the
  life sat at food ≥ 17 for the remaining 20k steps — vanilla drains
  only under work, so a calm sated body never got hungry again. The
  chain counter also shows a pairing artifact worth recording: the
  five eats followed ONE distant collect that landed just outside the
  600-step pairing window of the last eat. The substantive fact is
  the satiation plateau, not the window.
- **D3 pairs [off, off, off]**: five of six lives (including ALL
  THREE gated) read zero eats; the only expression was UNGATED life
  2. The gate's coupling site — a ≤ κ_d = 0.1 bonus on the label read
  *inside fired completions* — cannot create initiation; it can only
  amplify a chain that already fired. Against a per-life expression
  probability of roughly 0.3, a completion-site amplifier is
  invisible. No positive differential exists at this dose and site.

## The finding, stated for the design conversation

Initiation is the wall. A starving body two visible blocks from food
walks there and digs in ~1 of 3 lives — the chain triggers on
exploration-driven encounters, not on hunger. Hunger currently
touches action selection ONLY inside completions and recipe
selection; it never reaches the *initiation* of the taught chain.
Whatever fixes that (deficit-gated valuation at the drive site, a
hunger-aimed hold toward sensed food, or something better) is new
machinery, and per this topic's own registration it needs its own
registration with the owner. The senses stay: they moved "never" to
"sometimes" and gave every future gate a bell that rings.
