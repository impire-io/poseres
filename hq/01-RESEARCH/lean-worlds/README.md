# Lean worlds — when the world is too lean for two, does contention bite in the meters, and does an attribution sense earn its license through outcomes?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-17

## Abstract

Contested-worlds (episode 0109) refuted the learnability premise for
multi-body senses: on a renewable three-patch world, a second body —
forager or adversary — made the subject's world *more* predictable
(−37.9% / −25.3%) while its meters stayed healthy, and the topic
graduated abandoned with the successor premise written into its
reversal condition. This topic runs that successor: scarcity. On a
world calibrated to the leanest configuration a solo life still
survives, a hostile peer competes for food that does not come back.
The question moves from the head to the meters — eats, below-12 time,
starvation — and the attribution sense, if it earns a license at all,
earns it by recovering *outcomes*, not prediction error. A decisive
answer either finally demonstrates the co-habitation cost the
multi-body line presupposes, or closes that line for these worlds on
two topics' worth of evidence.

## The question

On the leanest solo-survivable world, does a hostile peer drive the
subject's survival meters down (below-12 time ≥ 3× solo, or
starvation loss), and does an instrument-grade peers sense recover a
measured share of that damage?

## The ladder

1. **L0 — calibrate the lean world** (solo arms only; config frozen
   before any paired arm).
2. **L1 — the hostile peer on the frozen world** (the reopening
   evidence 0109 named). PASS licenses the peers sense.
3. **L2 — the peers sense at instrument grade** (the 0109 trail's
   `peers_reading.py`, restored).
4. **L3 — outcomes with the sense**: fresh-taught 94-dim brain, its
   own solo baseline, the same hostile world.

## Pre-registered bars

Common protocol (the 0109 rig, restored from git history): live
Minecraft only, tick rate 100 / 50 ms, engine seed 1, blessed stack
exactly, hungry-born free roam, 3 segments × 67 cycles × 75 steps
per arm, views persisted, every peer act logged. **Meter window:**
segments 2–3 only (the hungry-born recovery transient of segment 1
is excluded; measured 0109: solo steady-state ge12 ≈ 1.0 on the
abundant world). "Below-12" = fraction of window steps with food
< 12/20.

- **Bar L0 — the lean world stands:** walking DOWN a declared ladder
  of configs (from three patches toward one patch, fewer pre-grown
  melons, fewer stems — the exact rungs recorded in JOURNEY.md before
  the walk), the frozen config is the leanest whose solo life shows
  zero starvation health-loss, ≥ 3 detected eats, and steady-state
  below-12 ≤ 0.30 (headroom so a 3× rise is measurable). One life per
  rung; the walk is part of the record.
- **Bar L1 — contention bites:** on the frozen config, the hostile
  peer (0109's policy, unchanged) drives the subject to below-12
  ≥ 3× its solo-lean value or ≥ 0.90 absolute, or to starvation
  health-loss where solo-lean had none. PASS licenses rung 2 by the
  senses-first rule.
- **Bar L2 — instrument-grade sense:** the restored peers reading
  passes all structural checks (presence, distance scale and cap,
  bearing rotating with the body's own turn, count, signature
  stability across reconnects, distinctness across names, sha256
  match) on the PEERS=1 stack before any behavior arm.
- **Bar L3 — the sense pays in outcomes:** a fresh-taught 94-dim
  brain (same 45-lesson recipe, no peer in any classroom), measured
  against its own solo-lean baseline, recovers ≥ 50% of the L1
  below-12 gap when run against the same hostile peer. Prediction
  error is recorded for the trail but claims nothing (0109's lesson).

## Reversal condition

- **L1 FAILS** (the peer cannot hurt the subject even at the
  calibrated edge of survivability): the co-habitation-cost premise
  dies at scarcity too. The topic graduates abandoned carrying the
  second null, and the multi-body sense line rests until a world
  demonstrates a cost — two topics of evidence that presence is not
  conflict in these worlds.
- **L3 FAILS after L1 PASSES** (the sense is instrument-grade, the
  damage is real, the meters do not recover): the sense-first design
  has measurably failed its gate — which, per the senses-first rule,
  is the one licensed moment for machinery. The topic graduates to
  design carrying the failure numbers, not abandoned.
- Standing guard, inherited from 0109: no claim about what the bodies
  are doing to each other enters JOURNEY.md without a channel reading
  behind it.
