# Recombination — can fragments compose into a path never demonstrated?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

The recipe stack teaches wholes: a demonstrated sequence is followed
end to end (episodes 0076–0084). The long-named successor (Doc 0010)
asks for means-ends: given fragments — each demonstrated separately,
the whole never shown — can the brain compose them? This is E2.1's
purist road continued: the teachers taught the steps; can the student
splice steps from different lessons? The owner licensed the topic
("start the recombination topic"). Staged honestly: **RC1** measures
whether the shipped machinery already composes (the menu's rotation
plus the itch's opportunism might stumble through a seam — nobody has
measured it); only if it does not, **RC2** licenses a minimal splice
mechanism (one-step value backup over a fragment graph,
instrument-grade). The meter stays OFF — one mechanism per gate.

## The question

With fragment A (travel to the crystal field, collect) and fragment B
(a handed-ingredient lesson: refine at the refinery) demonstrated
separately and the full journey never shown, do the shipped policies
produce refined crystal — and if not, does a one-step means-ends
backup over the fragment graph?

## Pre-registered bars

- **Bar RC1 — the shipped machinery composes:** ≥ **12/24** F1 seeds
  gain ≥ 1 refined within H (F0 floor published). At or above the
  bar: composition is already emergent, RC2 unnecessary — that is a
  finding, not a disappointment.
- **Bar RC2 — the splice composes** (licensed only if RC1 < 12/24):
  ≥ **18/24** F2 seeds refined, AND splice-led in ≥ half of the
  refining seeds (the first refine occurs while B is selected,
  after an A-attributed crystal acquisition).
- **Bar RC3 — composition does not eat the life:** in the passing
  arm, crystal acquisition (the ongoing work) ≥ the floor arm's
  median — composing must add the second leg, not cannibalize the
  first.

## Reversal condition

If RC1 fails and RC2 also fails with the instrument verified honest
(fragments captured with gains, the enable-edge detected, pointer
advancing on both legs), then one-step value backup over demonstrated
fragments is refuted at this horizon: composition needs deeper
machinery (multi-step backup, or the imagination layer), and that
becomes the named successor at Doc 0005's motivation seam **[O]** —
with these numbers as its motivation.

## Verdict

**Bar RC1 PASS at ceiling [measured, 2026-08-10, 24 seeds]: the
shipped machinery composes — the splice (RC2) was never licensed.**

- **RC1: 24/24** seeds gained refined (bar 12; floor 1/24), and not
  marginally — median 387 crystals and hundreds of refined per
  life, first refines typically within 500–900 steps. Composed-led
  23/24 (the first refine occurs with fragment B selected, after an
  A-attributed crystal) `[measured]`.
- **RC3: PASS**, with a recorded weakness: the floor's crystal
  median is 0 (drive + itch alone barely collect), so the bar as
  frozen is trivially cleared; the substantive reading is that the
  composing arm sustains first-leg work at scale (387 median) while
  adding the second leg `[measured]`.
- **How it composes** `[mechanism-argument]`: no planner appears —
  rotation (novelty) carries the bot between fragment territories,
  the pocket carries the *state* across the seam, and fragment B's
  terminal only wins selection when its precondition happens to be
  satisfied enough for progress. The fragments find each other
  through the world, not through lookahead.
- **Limits, stated plainly**: this is one-seam composition with
  pocket state as the bridge. Deeper chains (3+ fragments),
  non-pocket preconditions, and adversarial layouts are unmeasured.
  And the arena surfaced a structural finding recorded pre-run:
  **the acquisition-keyed itch is blind to value-preserving
  conversions** (1-for-1 refine is invisible; the arena yields 2,
  the C1 crafting precedent) — a named limit for any future economy
  with pure transformations.

Reversal condition unfired (both stages would have had to fail).
Successors named, none licensed: fragment-depth ladder (3+ seams);
conversion-visibility (an itch or extraction term for
transformations, not just acquisitions).
