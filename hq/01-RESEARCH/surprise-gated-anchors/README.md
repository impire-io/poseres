# Surprise-gated anchors — can mobility priced by evidence pass the wall that mobility priced by step could not?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-23

## Abstract

Factored-actions (episode 0112) closed with a measured tension:
anchors that start correct lose parity by moving (three editions
bracketed the F0 line while every frozen sibling passed ~5/1k
higher), yet exceptions — the is/was/went regime language cannot
avoid — require anchors that move. Design 0019's constraint 2 names
the candidate this topic runs: **anchor movement gated on
accumulated surprise** — a lawful act's error converges and its
anchor freezes itself; an exceptional act's error stays high and
keeps its mobility. If the gate passes lawful parity with mobility
ON and absorbs a declared fraction of exceptional acts while the
untried-equals-practiced property holds, the blocked Bar F4 finally
reads and the language gate's action side gains its exception
mechanism. If the gate cannot separate the regimes, the tension is
deeper than pricing, and design 0019 records that hierarchical
mechanisms are next.

## The question

Does gating anchor movement on per-act accumulated surprise pass
lawful-world parity (where per-step mobility failed three times)
while absorbing exceptional acts (where pinned anchors cannot), on
the dial world, under the 0112 protocol?

## The mechanism (declared before it is built)

The v2 product anchors and machinery of 0112, restored from its
trail, with one addition: a per-act prediction-error EMA `E_a`
(α = 0.1) beside the store's global error EMA `G`. The anchor row
`a` receives its (clipped) gradient **only while `E_a > θ·G`**
(θ = 2, declared; both dials amendable openly pre-arm with the
numbers that force it). Lawful acts converge below the gate and
freeze themselves; exceptional acts sit above it and stay mobile.
No other change: same world, same 13,000-step budget, same frozen
F0 thresholds, same probe, same completable-target rule, event head
at the shipped η = 0.5 in special arms, all rig-side, the one-hot
parity self-test re-run before any arm.

## Pre-registered bars

Protocol inherited from 0112 unchanged (dial world B=4, 24 seeds,
budget and F0 thresholds as frozen there; the F4 world = mask 10% +
disjoint irregular 10% at m=256, probes with per-act arrays).

- **Bar SG0 — lawful parity with mobility ON:** at m=3, gated
  mobility passes the frozen F0 clauses (median ≥ 10.385, min >
  6.923, pred_late ≤ 0.803) — the wall that lr 0.01 and 0.001 both
  hit.
- **Bar SG1 — exceptions absorbed, the lawful preserved:** on the
  F4 world at m=256, the gated arm's irregular-act event-head error
  lands within **2×** its own regular-act error, while its held-out
  regular error stays within **1.25×** its regular error (untried ≡
  practiced, maintained under exceptions). Margins relative to the
  arm's own trained error — the 0112 margin lesson applied.
- **Bar SG2 — the gate is what did it:** the frozen-v2 control on
  the same F4 world FAILS SG1's irregular clause (its pinned
  anchors cannot fit exceptions). Discrimination measured, not
  assumed.

## Reversal condition

- **SG0 fails:** evidence-priced mobility hits the same wall as
  step-priced — the parity cost is not about pricing, and the
  tension is structural. The topic graduates back into design 0019
  as a strengthened constraint (mobility incompatible with this
  conditioning; hierarchical or gate-per-slot mechanisms named
  next), abandoned only if no design content was added.
- **SG1 fails with SG0 passing:** the gate protects parity but
  cannot absorb exceptions (frozen-in-practice); same routing — the
  constraint sharpens.
- **SG2 fails** (frozen absorbs exceptions too): the F4 world's
  exceptions are too easy as declared; amend the exceptional
  fraction/permutation openly with the numbers before re-running.
- Standing guard: no comparison-arm claim without its thresholds
  frozen before the arm ran; instrument readings stay trail.

## Verdict

Graduated **to design**, 2026-08-23, on the owner's call — all
three bars PASS; the mechanism folds into design
[0019](../../02-DESIGN/0019-factored-actions.md) as constraint 2's
measured answer. Episode 0114.

- **Bar SG0 — PASS** [measured]: gated 11.692 / 15.077 / 22.769,
  pred_late 0.566 against the frozen thresholds — at the frozen
  arm's own level (15.538), where three step-priced editions
  bracketed 10.2–10.6. Gate moves median 23.5 per lawful life: the
  anchors freeze themselves where the world is lawful.
- **Bar SG1 (amended) — PASS 24/24 on both clauses** [measured]:
  on the slot-breaking world, irregular/regular median 1.559 (max
  1.905, every seed ≤ 2×); held-out/regular median 1.002 (max
  1.154, every seed ≤ 1.25×) — exceptions absorbed with untried ≡
  practiced intact. Gate moves median 1,104: mobility spent where
  surprise accumulates.
- **Bar SG2 (amended) — PASS** [measured]: the frozen-v2 control
  fails the irregular clause 21/24 (irr/reg median 2.379) with its
  lawful transfer intact — the discrimination is the gate's, not
  the world's. Amendment 1 (slot-breaking exceptions) was
  registered with the first edition's numbers before any amended
  arm ran: the original exceptions were absorbable by
  channel-dilution and mean-shift (frozen irr/reg 1.573, 19/24
  inside the clause it had to fail); the first-edition pair is
  trail (-exc1 files).

Load-bearing claims: **mobility priced by evidence passes the wall
that mobility priced by step could not** — same anchors, same lr,
the gate alone differs [measured]; the gate's economy is the
mechanism: ~23 moves per lawful life vs ~1,100 on the exception
world [measured]; the absolute mobility premium persists (gated
lawful error 0.110 vs frozen 0.060, ~1.8×) and is stated beside
the relative bars, which are margined against own error per the
0112 lesson [measured]; exception difficulty must break the SLOT to
discriminate mechanisms — value-only exceptions dilute into
channel averages [measured, Amendment 1's justification]. Confounds
as journaled: one world geometry; dials α=0.1, θ=2 as declared and
never swept; the premium unchased (a named door, not a bar).
