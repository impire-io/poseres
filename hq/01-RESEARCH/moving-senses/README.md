# Moving senses — a world that changes without you

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-11

## Abstract

Every sense the stack has ever been measured against was stationary,
or changed only because the brain acted. The owner named the generic
capability hiding inside "the market" (2026-08-11): **senses that
change out of the brain's control** — first drifting on their own,
then *reacting* to the brain's behavior (the world learning you
back). Per the senses-first rule (recorded in
`hq/00-GENESIS/how-we-work.md` the same day), no new machinery is on
the table: the moving quantity is simply one more channel, and the
gate asks whether the existing head, tongue, and worth-keyed
machinery track and exploit it. The market — a trader whose exchange
rate moves — is the instantiation, not the subject.

## The question

With the trader's rate published as a sensed channel and moving
outside the brain's control — oscillating between bargain and ruin —
does the shipped stack keep trading, learn *when* to trade (buying
low without any timing machinery, purely through the head's
context-dependent worth predictions), and sustain trade when its own
behavior worsens the rate?

## Instrument (frozen with the bars)

- **The moving-rate world** (the learned-worth body of episode 0089,
  one change): the trade costs **r(t) crystals per gem**, with
  r(t) = round(8 + 6·sin(2π(t + φ)/2000)) ∈ [2, 14], published on
  the (repurposed) channel 13 as r/16. The gem's felt meal stays
  1.0 and crystal worth 0.1, so **break-even is r = 10**: below it a
  trade gains worth, above it a trade *destroys* worth. Timing is
  therefore not an efficiency nicety — the top of the cycle is
  ruinous, and the worth-keyed itch can only refuse it if the head
  learns the rate channel's bearing on the trade's outcome (the
  head is linear over all channels — this is exactly within its
  reach, and exactly what has never been measured).
- **Teaching with witnessed variety**: the 0089 protocol, but each
  segment's rate phase is offset (φ = 167 · segment), so the
  lessons sample the whole rate cycle — including ruinous trades.
  Recorded design choice: the teacher demonstrates the *act*; the
  body feels the worth; the head needs witnessed variety, not
  curated success. Handed trade lessons carry 14 crystals (the
  worst-case price).
- **Arms** (24 seeds, H = 5,000): **N0** fixed r = 3 (the 0089
  anchor re-measured on this cohort); **N1** the drifting rate;
  **N2** the reactive rate — base r = 3 plus a markup that jumps +2
  on every trade and decays with τ = 500 ticks (capped at 14): the
  world responds to the brain and to nothing else.
- **Pilot** seeds 1–8 (N1 + N2), published before any 24-seed arm:
  rate channel verified sensed, teaching variety verified, trade
  mechanics at variable r verified.

## Pre-registered bars

- **MS1 — life under motion:** N1 seeds with ≥ 1 gem ≥ **18/24**
  (the economy survives a moving world).
- **MS2 — timing without timing machinery:** in N1, the median rate
  actually paid across all trades < **8.0** (the time-average rate —
  strictly better than indifference) AND ruinous trades (r > 10) ≤
  **10%** of all trades.
- **MS3 — the world reacts, the brain paces:** N2 seeds with ≥ 3
  gems ≥ **12/24** — sustained trading when every trade worsens the
  price, which demands spacing no lesson ever showed.

Registered readings: the trades-vs-rate histogram (the demand curve
the brain expresses); refusal evidence (completion firings vs rate);
book stability under motion; the N0 anchor row.

## Reversal condition

If MS2 fails with the rate channel verified sensed and the teaching
variety verified witnessed, the linear event head cannot carry
context-dependent outcome predictions at this horizon — the first
measured limit of senses-first — and a richer predictor (context
terms at the head's seam, Doc 0005) becomes the named successor,
licensed by these numbers.
