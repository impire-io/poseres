# Episode 0112 — Factored actions: untried equals practiced, and the interaction is the factoring (2026-08-21 → 2026-08-22)

The language gate's second prerequisite (episode 0042), run as the
owner's chosen focus item — substance before showcase. The question:
can the kernel's action side carry ~10³ acts via embeddings over
factored anchors, matching flat where flat is validated, surviving
where flat starves, and predicting acts never executed? The rig: a
dial world on the kernel's own EventSource seam (B=4 dials, m
positions, act = set dial to position — the stem × inflection
miniature), ladder revised before anything ran so only the act
inventory grows (A ∈ {12, 64, 256, 1024}, obs and horizon constant),
25 sweeps of 24 seeds each at a 13,000-step budget frozen with the
F0 thresholds before any comparison arm, machinery rig-side with a
one-hot parity proof (embedded ≡ flat to machine precision), and a
perfect-knowledge oracle pricing every rung's ceiling.

What measured true, in ladder order: flat performs at A=12 (12.6
reaches/1k vs the 6.9 random floor), drops 8× at A=64, and is
**dead at A=1024 — 0/24 seeds, ~12.7 samples per slice — Bar F1
PASS** while the oracle ceiling stays flat within 6%: starvation,
not task scaling. The hand-declared anchor [onehot(d); p] beat flat
at A=12 then collapsed terminally from A=64 on; its failure
diagnosed itself when the F3 probe read the learned arm 2.49×
WORSE than a blank slate on held-out AND executed acts alike: the
linear-in-embedding family over concatenated anchors spans rank
B+1 where the world's action family needs 2B [mechanism-argument,
corroborated by the v1 F4 pair's uniformly flat errors]. The
second-edition anchor made the product explicit —
[onehot(d); onehot(d)·p] — and delivered the topic's substantial
result [measured]: **a never-executed act predicts exactly as well
as a constantly-executed one** (held-out 0.0409 ≡ regular 0.0414 ≈
flat-after-training 0.0375, 86% median per-act wins over cold).
Bar F3 still FAILS as registered — its 0.5×-cold clause demands
what flat's own trained slices (0.69× cold) cannot do; measured
overreach in the margin, reported, never amended post-hoc.

What was refuted or reversed: the registered expectation that
retention was reachable (Bar F2 FAIL for every arm and edition,
original and pre-arm-amended forms — the embedding buys survival
and relative advantage, learned > flat > frozen from m=16 on,
never retention); the drift-noise story for the learned arm (three
editions bracket the F0 line while frozen reads 14.5–15.5; a 10×
rate cut recovered 0.154 of a ~5/1k gap — **mobility and lawful
parity are in tension**, Bar F4 BLOCKED with surprise-gated anchor
movement as the named door); and the assumption that representation
explains behavior — frozen-v2's exact anchors predict perfectly
and win nothing (reach 0/24 from m=64 up) while rank-broken
learned-v1 reached: **prediction and behavior dissociate at the
action seam** (jitter-as-exploration hypothesis [judgment],
discriminator named, unrun).

What it taught / what it opened: the interaction term is what
"factored" means — for language, "this ending on this stem," never
stem and ending side by side; generalization bars must be margined
against trained-knowledge ceilings, not cold baselines, in worlds
with sparse deltas; instrument lessons banked (the playerless-tick
server fact has a sibling here: the F0 parity gate caught every
machinery edition before it could waste a ladder). Design
[0019](../02-DESIGN/0019-factored-actions.md) carries the
constraint map a successor action-side mechanism must satisfy; the
teacher-world experiment now has its action-side prerequisite
measured — one of 0042's three gates.

Reversal condition: a mechanism that passes lawful parity with
mobility on (surprise-gated or otherwise) reopens Bar F4's question
inside a fresh registration; a world where flat demonstrably
carries 10³ acts at a comparable budget retracts the F1 ceiling
claim; and if a successor's held-out prediction beats the
trained-knowledge ceiling itself, design 0019's margin guidance
tightens accordingly.
