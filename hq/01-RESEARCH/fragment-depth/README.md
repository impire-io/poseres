# Fragment depth — where does free composition run out?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-10

## Abstract

Episode 0086 measured one-seam composition as emergent: fragments
taught separately compose 24/24, glued by rotation, the pocket, and
the itch — no planner. The mechanism is luck-shaped: wander between
known territories while the pocket carries state. That cannot scale
forever; somewhere there is a rung where wander-and-carry stops
lining the legs up in order and something must look ahead. This
topic measures the decay curve rung by rung and, if a rung fails,
licenses the minimal splice (the one-step value backup registered
but never needed in the recombination topic) at exactly the failing
rung. The boundary is the deliverable: below it, teaching fragments
suffices; above it, planning earns its keep.

## The question

With a four-stage process chain (collect m0 → process to m1 → m2 →
m3 at three distinct stations), every fragment taught separately and
no chain ever demonstrated, how deep does emergent composition reach
in one life — and does the one-step splice extend the failing rung?

## Instrument (frozen with the bars)

- **The ladder body** (harness anatomy, 16-dim, meter off): the
  sample-field movement/collect mechanics with a material chain —
  the field (2, 2) collects m0; stations P1 (10, 10), P2 (10, 2),
  P3 (2, 10) each transform one unit of their input into **two** of
  the next material (the measured gains-not-swaps rule, Doc 0010).
  Channels: per-material counts, pocket total, positions at (9, 10)
  on /12, progress, station kind. Legs are 8–16 steps.
- **Fragments** (handed-ingredient teaching, the 0086 protocol):
  A collect-m0; B1 handed m0 → process at P1; B2 handed m1 → P2;
  B3 handed m2 → P3. Cohort: 24 seeds × 48 interleaved segments
  (12 passes each); no chain demonstration anywhere.
- **Arms** (24 seeds, H = 5,000): **G1** the shipped `RecipePolicy`
  with the four-fragment menu (the free-composition arm); **G2**
  (licensed per-rung only on a G1 rung failure) the splice — the
  recombination registration's one-step backup (`value(X) += γ ·
  drive_value(Y.terminal)` where X's terminal pocket satisfies Y's
  demonstrated starting pocket and the bot's does not; γ = 0.5).
- **Pilot** seeds 1–8, G1 only, published before the arms
  (fragments capture, stations transform, teaching green).

## Pre-registered bars

- **Bar L2 — depth 2 replicates:** ≥ **18/24** G1 seeds reach m1
  (episode 0086's anchor: 24/24 on its arena).
- **Bar L3 — depth 3:** ≥ **12/24** G1 seeds reach m2.
- **Bar L4 — depth 4:** ≥ **12/24** G1 seeds reach m3.
- **Bar LS — the splice extends the boundary** (licensed only at the
  first failing rung): G2 lifts that rung to ≥ **18/24**.

The registered deliverable is the decay curve (seeds reaching
m1/m2/m3, and how many units of each) whatever the bars say; a
failing rung is the boundary being *found*, not a disappointment.

## Reversal condition

If the first failing rung resists the splice too (LS fails with the
instrument verified honest — fragments captured, enable-edges
detected, pointers advancing), one-step backup is refuted at that
depth and multi-step backup / the imagination layer becomes the
named successor at Doc 0005's motivation seam, with the measured
boundary as its licensing evidence.

## Verdict

**ALL THREE RUNGS PASS [measured, 2026-08-10, 24 seeds]: the ladder
holds to depth 4 — the boundary was not found.**

- **Decay curve: m0 24/24 → m1 22/24 → m2 21/24 → m3 20/24** (bars
  18/12/12). Twenty seeds walk the complete four-leg
  never-demonstrated chain; production is real at every stage
  (unit medians 279 / 54 / 38 / 20) `[measured]`.
- **The splice (G2) was never licensed** — wander-and-carry with
  the pocket as the state bridge loses roughly one seed per rung,
  a gentle slope, not a cliff. The "luck runs out" intuition is
  refuted at this depth and geometry `[measured]`.
- Bounds, plainly: one geometry (8–16-step legs, inexhaustible
  stations, yield-2 transforms), depth 4, H = 5,000. The boundary
  exists somewhere — longer legs, scarcer inputs, or deeper chains
  will find it — but it is beyond everything measured here
  `[judgment]`.

Reversal condition unfired. Successor named, unlicensed: the
boundary hunt (depth 5+, scarce inputs, adversarial geometry) —
worth running only when a real embodiment demands it.
