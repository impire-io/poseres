# The opaque world — does composition pay where position must be carried, not sensed?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-25

## Abstract

Episode 0118 measured the compositional gate on the frozen echo-teacher
world and the tower won on parsimony — but that world hands sequence
position to the senses through its progress channel, which is the
special case, not the general one (the owner's observation,
2026-08-25). Real worlds are position-opaque: Minecraft's forage chain
(see the melon, walk, dig, collect, hold, eat) has no channel anywhere
saying "you are at step 4 of the plan." 0118's own reversal condition
names exactly this arena. This topic runs the relocated comparison on
live Minecraft — the survival rig's probe world: a real server, real
mineflayer body, real food economy, provisioned melon patches for
measurability (the house's established live-measurement standard;
nothing faked) — with wild-world C1 adoption the named follow-on if
the mechanism earns it. It also measures the snapshot-topology
lifecycle cost 0117 named: references must survive the teach→life
serialization boundary or the primitive's price is on the record.

## The question

On live Minecraft, where plan position must be carried rather than
sensed, does a composition tier improve the taught forage life — and
does the identity-bound reference form (0118's loser on the
transparent world) beat the stacked tower there, as 0118's reversal
condition predicts it could?

## Reversal condition

If the flat arm fails to reproduce d23-class foraging at all (zero
eats across its 8 lives), the rig is re-validated before any composed
reading — an instrument failure, not a verdict. If the protocol
proves too noisy to discriminate (every pairwise arm difference on
every meter inside one life-spread SE at n = 8), the protocol scales
or re-scopes openly rather than declaring a null. And the standing
condition this topic exists to test: a reference win here reverses
0118's shape verdict for position-opaque worlds; a reference loss
here extends it, and the tower's parsimony claim graduates from
lab-bound to measured-in-the-wild.

## Verdict

- **M0 PASS** [measured]: tier-2 map rate ≥ 0.9986 through 135
  lessons and 32 composed lives; the arbiter held the predictor
  31–52% of calls in every composed life (clause asked 5% once); the
  composed state (blob + sidecar) resumed byte-exactly on the cheap
  world and round-tripped byte-identically at all 90 composed lesson
  checkpoints. The lifecycle rode along as a result: ~74 orphan
  events per bind-pred life, ~26 of ~39 references dangling at end,
  population never destabilized — 0117's eviction fear measured
  benign under graceful degradation.
- **M1 FAIL to demonstrate, precisely: indistinguishable**
  [measured]: at the locked n = 16, no composed arm beats flat on
  total eats (flat 41, bind-pred 38, tower 25); bind − flat is dead
  even (−0.19 ± 0.64 SE). Composition neither helps nor hurts the
  wild forage life detectably at this dose.
- **M2 FAIL as registered, verdict as locked: indistinguishable at
  protocol scale** [measured]: bind − tower +0.81 ± 0.91 SE
  (t = +0.89, 8 wins / 2 losses / 6 ties paired) — no beyond-spread
  separation. Recorded alongside, sub-spread and not leaned on: the
  transparent world's winner is nominally LAST on every behavioral
  meter (tower 4/16 lives eating vs 8–9/16), and bind-pred doubles
  flat's forage chains (6 vs 3). The lab ordering did not transfer.
- The noise clause fired once at n = 8 and the protocol scaled to
  n = 16 as registered; the n = 16 verdict landed as locked, with
  the raw rows in the rig.

Mechanism account [judgment]: wild behavior is carried by the
recipe/itch stack and the event head; frames speak only through
drive valuation of one-step predictions, so the composed tiers
changed who holds the predictor without changing the life. The
forage chain is position-opaque but short-horizon (~60–100 steps
per chain) under heavy policy scaffolding — the deep-sequence
demand that motivated the gate never binds these meters. The
position-opaque long-horizon world is the named successor.
