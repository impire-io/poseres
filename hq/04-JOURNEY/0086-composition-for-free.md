# Episode 0086 — Composition for free: the fragments find each other (2026-08-10)

The arc's longest-named successor finally ran ("start the
recombination topic"): fragments demonstrated separately — A, walk
to the crystal field and collect; B, a handed-ingredient lesson,
walk to the refinery and refine — with the two-leg journey never
shown end to end. The staged registration asked first whether the
shipped machinery *already* composes (RC1), licensing a splice
mechanism (RC2) only on failure. The arena: the sample-field body
with a refinery station, meter off, one seam, the pocket as the only
bridge between the lessons.

**It composes at ceiling [measured]: RC1 24/24** (bar 12; floor
1/24), median 387 crystals and hundreds of refined per life, first
refines within ~500–900 steps, fragment-led 23/24. RC2 was never
licensed. The never-demonstrated journey isn't found once and
memorized — it is walked continuously, as a rhythm.

How, without a planner [mechanism-argument]: rotation (the drive's
boredom, episode 0080) moves the bot between fragment territories;
the pocket carries state across the seam; and fragment B's ending
attracts selection while the completion itch can only actually
finish it when the precondition is met. Composition emerges from
menu dynamics plus world state — the same three mechanisms already
shipped, meeting at a seam.

Two findings recorded before any run, both design-relevant:

- **"Meter off" must silence the felt channel too** — an instrument
  discipline note.
- **The acquisition-keyed itch is blind to value-preserving
  conversion**: a 1-crystal → 1-refined refine never changes the
  pocket total, so neither the shipped extraction nor the itch can
  see it. The arena's refine yields two (the C1 crafting precedent —
  every measured chain *gains*). This is a real structural limit:
  economies built on pure transformations need a
  conversion-visibility term that does not exist yet.

passed trivially because the floor's crystal median is 0 — the bar
as frozen was weak; the substantive fact is first-leg work sustained
at scale (387 median) while composing. Limits stated plainly:
one-seam composition, pocket-state bridge; deeper chains and
non-pocket preconditions are unmeasured.

What it opens (named, unlicensed): the fragment-depth ladder (3+

Reversal condition: none — records a completed measurement. A future

shape failing at 3+ seams) does not reverse this result; it bounds
