# Episode 0113 — Separated patches: the war is at the birth minute, not between the patches (2026-08-23)

Design 0017's last axis-2 arm, opened from 0111's reversal and run
in one day as the parallel autonomous thread: with the larder split
across two distant patches, does separation open the partial gap
(hostile-86 below-12 in 0.10–0.90) that larder size and renewal
rate never did? The 0111 rig restored onto its own world
(`pgw2-minecraft`), two patches of C-1's total-war count each (2+2
non-renewing melons), distance the one declared variable
(D12/D24/D48, walk order and expectations journaled before
anything ran), the 0110 protocol otherwise unchanged.

What measured true: **Bar S0 PASS at every rung** — solo below-12
0.000 with 6/7/3 eats; at D48 the blind body found and used a
larder two glance-horizons from the other, at exactly the 3-eat
minimum. **Bar S1 FAIL everywhere** — hostile-86 read 1.000 at
every distance, 0 eats, 0 collects, food to 2 with health untouched
(hunger's edge, the 0110 signature). Bar S2 never reached. The act
logs carry the mechanism [measured]: at D12/D24 the peer's four
digs consumed the entire split larder; at D48 the peer dug **only
patch A's two melons — patch B's larder stood untouched for the
whole life — and the subject still ate nothing.** Distance cannot
open a middle that the birth minute closes: the contest is decided
at the near patch in the peer's first digs (0110's two-dig war,
unchanged by geometry), and a hungry-born subject that lost its
opening never expresses the far journey even when nothing contests
it — the D48 solo's own 3-eat knife edge shows that journey is
marginal even undisturbed.

What was refuted: the topic's premise — that WHERE the rival stands
could decide WHAT is contested, making travel time the resource a
camper cannot confiscate. It cannot, at this life length and peer
speed, because the deciding contest never leaves the first patch.
With rate (episode 0111) and separation (here) both null, **design
0017's axis 2 is closed on both arms** — the third registered null
in the multi-body line — and the design's last standing candidate
is comparable-speed bodies (axis 3): a rival that races at a taught
brain's own reaction time, where birth-minute wars are symmetric.

What it taught / what it opened: the birth-minute transient is
load-bearing in every contest result this line has produced — a
future registration that seats the subject fed, or measures from a
mid-life baseline, would separate "cannot win races" from "cannot
recover from a lost opening" [judgment — a named registration
choice, not a flaw in this one: the hungry-born protocol was the
declared inheritance]. The two-patch rig and its distance ladder
join the instrument estate in the trail.

Reversal condition: a measured hostile-86 below-12 steady-state in
0.10–0.90 on any axis-2 world — including protocols that decouple
the birth minute (fed-born or mid-life windows), registered fresh —
reopens the partial-gap hunt; absent that, the peers sense's
outcome license waits on axis 3.
