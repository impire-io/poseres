# 0017 — Lean worlds: when is another body worth sensing?

**Status:** design (2026-08-17). Born from research topics
contested-worlds (episode 0109) and lean-worlds (episode 0110) — the
one licensed moment for machinery under the senses-first rule: a
sense-first design, made instrument-grade, measurably failed its
outcome gate. Every claim below carries the measurement that taught
it; the design question is stated functionally, explicit enough for
`/speckit-specify` when an axis is picked.

## What is measured and settled (the floor this stands on)

- **Presence is not conflict.** On renewable worlds a second body —
  generous or adversarial — *improved* the subject's prediction
  error (−37.9% / −25.3%) and left its meters healthy; the only
  interference fingerprint (mining, the contended channel) was an
  order too small for any bar (0109).
- **Scarcity bites all-or-nothing.** On non-renewing larders the
  hostile-arm cost flips from total (2 melons: below-12 steady-state
  1.000, zero eats, the war over in the peer's first 2 digs) to zero
  (3 and 4 melons: 0.000) with nothing between. Contests at this
  peer speed are decided by race, not information (0110).
- **The peers sense exists, opt-in, instrument-grade.** Bridge
  `PEERS=1` + `c1_anatomy(peers=True)` → 94/13: nearest other body
  as presence, egocentric bearing, distance, count, sha256 username
  signature — the drops grammar exactly; Bar L2 27/27 on the
  self-locating reading (`examples/minecraft/survival/arms/
  peers_reading.py`). **Promotion into the default anatomy is NOT
  licensed** — the sense has never touched an outcome.
- **Expression latency decides birth-minute wars.** Two teach draws
  of the same 45-lesson recipe differed by one segment of free-roam
  expression latency, and that difference decided every race either
  brain was in (0110's solo94 wobble; D3's measured latency band).
- **No brain has ever been taught about peers.** Every classroom in
  the record was clean-roomed solitary; the peers channel read zeros
  through all 45 lessons. Whatever taught peer-dynamics could pay is
  unexplored.

## The design question

**What makes information about another body worth something in
outcomes?** A sense pays only where behavior has slack — where
knowing changes what the body can *do* about it. Three candidate
axes, ordered by senses-first parsimony (cheapest and least
machinery first); each is a future pre-registered topic, not a
build order:

1. **Teaching (no new machinery).** Social lessons: a scripted peer
   present in the classroom, the pupil taught chains that condition
   on the peers channel — collect-near-a-competitor, leave-when-
   contested, go-where-the-rival-is-not. The cheapest decisive test:
   a peer-taught brain re-runs the hostile arms of a partial-gap
   world and must close ≥ 50% of the blind gap. Prerequisite: a
   partial-gap world exists (axis 2 supplies it).
2. **World geometry (the world, not the brain).** The unexplored
   middle: spatially separated patches so WHERE the rival is changes
   WHERE to forage; coordination artifacts as world facts (claims —
   deferred from 0109) belong here. Functional requirement: a
   declared config whose hostile-86 below-12 steady-state lands in
   **0.10–0.90**. *The renewal-rate arm is measured down* (episode
   0111, 2026-08-18): on the one-patch geometry, regrowth at every
   declared rate read 1.000 — the shadowing peer camps where renewal
   lands, so supply cannot open a middle; what remains of this axis
   is separation, not rate.
3. **Comparable-speed bodies.** The scripted adversary acts at
   script speed against a taught brain's reaction time; two brains
   (a C1-style pair) make races fair without faking anything. This
   is the original rung-4 territory (population worlds) and the most
   expensive axis; it waits until axes 1–2 have verdicts.

## Constraints carried forward (measured, not negotiable)

- **No telepathy**: bodies sense world-visible facts only; peer
  telemetry is never a sense (held through both topics).
- **Properties, not names**: identity enters as the appearance
  signature (proven stable across reconnects, distinct across
  names, sha256-exact at L2).
- **Instrument bars before behavior bars**; live world only; the
  meters carry outcome claims — prediction error is trail, never a
  bar (the two-topic lesson).
- **Meter windows exclude the hungry-born transient** (segments 2–3;
  0110's registered operationalization).

## Definition of done (functional; a follow-up feature specs from here)

1. A declared world config with a measured partial blind gap
   (hostile-86 below-12 steady-state in 0.10–0.90, one life, the
   0110 protocol).
2. One intervention from axis 1 or 2, pre-registered, that closes
   ≥ 50% of that gap against its own solo baseline (which must pass
   L0), with the peer's acts logged and views persisted.
3. The sense's promotion question re-opens ONLY on such a measured
   win; until then `peers=True` stays an instrument flag.
