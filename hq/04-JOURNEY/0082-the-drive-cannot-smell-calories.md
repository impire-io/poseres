# Episode 0082 — The drive cannot smell calories (2026-08-10)

The owner's "survival of the fittest" instinct, made registrable: a
lean economy where payments differ 40× (sticks +0.15, cobble +0.004
per gain-tick, the G4b taper on the drain) over the ten-recipe menu,
asking whether survival pressure *alone* — no praise, no new wiring —
shifts choice toward what nourishes. The registered prediction said
no: the drive values novelty of endings and has no calorie sense.
A fresh 34-dim cohort (verdict + energy from birth) was built 24/24
green; the pilot was published before the arms and forced one open
amendment.

**The pilot's finding first, because it reshaped the topic:** the
plan used episode 0080's praise-command (β 0.5 on wall-center
cobble) to force a junk diet — and the diet refused to be forced.
The commanded bots' product mix was nearly identical to the free
menu's (sticks 46 vs 62 median units, planks 81 vs 56) because
**praise commands the selection pointer while the completion itch
commands the metabolism** — it crafts whatever the pocket holds
wherever the bot stands. With near-identical mixes no price table
can separate the arms, so the diet-differential bar E1 was replaced
openly (E1′: lean economy livable, ≥ 18/24 alive) with the raw
numbers recorded pre-arms [measured].

What it opens — and this is the episode's real product: the
**deficit→value coupling** is now a named, numbers-motivated design
question. Hunger must reach wanting; two candidate shapes are
recorded in Doc 0010's successor note (the deficit modulating the
drive's terminal valuation, or the deficit driving the label channel
— "hunger praises food"). Until such a layer exists, no amount of
meter pressure will steer what the brain chooses to do — only
whether it survives what it chose.

Reversal condition: none — records a completed measurement with its
prediction confirmed. A future composition showing V1-vs-V0
nourishing-share ratio ≥ 1.5× *without* an explicit deficit→value
