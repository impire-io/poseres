# Episode 0059 — Scheduled probing: the window closes before the probe arrives (2026-07-26)

Chapter 40 closed four passive signal families with one verdict — no
passive statistic separates a world change from the brain's own
nonstationarity; the detector's background is the brain itself — and
named the active successor: **scheduled probing**, deliberately
re-visit mastered ground under a held policy and re-test, detection as
a designed experiment. This topic ran that experiment in one night,
with every judgment frozen before any run: the P0/P1/P2 ladder, the 4×
and 2× brackets inherited from the passive program, the P1 threshold
θ = 3.0 (frozen from the registration alone, before the pilot), the
frontier-arm constraint, and a settledness criterion for the mastery
point. The instrument (scratchpad, zero `src/pra` edits — a world
wrapper restoring the recorded start state while preserving the shift
counter, a probe policy replaying the recorded 40-action tape, an
online-step error log) proved itself on four gates: state restored
exactly, tape replayed exactly, post-shift probes provably on the
shifted world, byte-identical reruns [measured]. The pilot passed —
episode-130 mastery settled in all seven screening worlds (ratios
0.92–1.45 vs ≤ 1.5) — so the protocol ran exactly as registered, no
amendments.

**P0 FAILed both clauses at the screening tier** [measured]. The first
post-shift probe — the earliest revisit the cadence allows — read
C = 0.84–1.76 across all six shift cells against the ≥ 4 bar. And the
benign clause broke from the other side: with world, start state, and
action tape all pinned, the brain's own churn alone moved benign probe
readings past the 2× clause (no-shift twin s3 to 2.41; multiregion s1
six times across 49 probes, to 2.80). **The benign band sits above the
entire shift band** — no threshold separates bands that have crossed;
θ was moot before it was reached. Confirmation tier not run
(registered read-only-on-pass); P1/P2 not run (conditional).

The mechanism, measured from the same captures: **relearning completes
inside one episode** (in the first shifted episode, within-episode
error medians collapse 0.21–0.64 early → 0.05–0.22 late; one dynamics
seed sat at floor within its first 40 shifted steps); **election
censoring survives the held policy** (emission arms halve their
electing census on the probe route, 14.0 → 6.5 mean electing frames,
while the surviving mean reads benign — the chapter-38 censor intact
under total route control); and the raw one-step error carries < 4× of
headroom over its own noise floor (baselines 0.07–0.19; the best
reading anywhere in these runs is 3.9×, gone by the next episode).

**Refuted:** the topic's own premise — that removing the
policy-movement confound is what passive reading lacked. The probe
controlled everything the premise asked for, and the reading did not
move. The registration decided against its author the same day it was
written, on its own bars — the house pattern holding at its fifth
consecutive gate this week.

**What it taught [judgment]:** chapter 40's verdict has a twin — the
detector's background is the brain itself, and *the detector's window
is the brain's own relearning speed*. The staleness signal lives
~10–30 steps; the earliest scheduled revisit arrives an episode late;
there is nothing left to detect by the time any probe can run. The
staleness-detection program is now closed on both its passive and
active sides — five signal families, one frozen testbed, one verdict.
The chapter-39 tolerant gate (~1.6% false-fire) remains the recorded
deployment option, and Doc 05's guidance never depended on detection:
competence stands. Named but not licensed (each needs its own
registered gate): a first-transfer-steps probe statistic (context rows
show episode-start noise may drown it), a censorship-proof per-frame
reference read (record the mastery frames' ids; read those frames on
revisit, electing or not), and event-triggered probing (which requires
the trigger chapters 39–40 already closed).

Reversal condition: the question reopens if (a) a brain configuration
measurably relearns slower than one episode on this testbed (the
transient outliving probe latency — e.g. a plasticity or protection
change lengthening the first-shifted-episode collapse), (b) one of the
named doors is picked and pre-registers its own gate, or (c) a
deployment surfaces a persistent unlearnable shift class these worlds
do not model — a transient that does not decay.
