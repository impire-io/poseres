# 0020 — Sequence worlds and the fixed-width sense

**Status:** design (2026-08-23). Born from research topic
sequence-encoding (episode 0115) — the teacher-world gate's third
prerequisite (episode 0042), measured and scoped. Every claim
carries its measurement; the open question is stated explicitly
enough for the next registration.

## What is measured and settled (the floor this stands on)

- **The kernel learns sequence worlds through body-declared senses,
  unmodified.** On the echo-teacher world (tokens emitted, a
  scripted teacher answering only through the observation,
  acceptance as redraw novelty, no reward wire), every declared
  encoding performed at 3.2–6.4× the random floor on the easy rung
  with the oracle at its exact arithmetic (0115, Bar Q0).
- **Structure–encoding affinity is real at comparable competence.**
  The decay summary (an m-channel recency sum — a soft counter)
  led the counting family outright on behavior (25.5/1k vs the
  best window's 19.7) and on teacher-anticipation (+0.052 pulse
  advantage), while placing mid-field on phase templates; window
  encodings show the mirrored profile (0115).
- **Width costs more than information at this scale.** Narrower
  encodings beat wider ones on behavior, conformity, and
  anticipation at nearly every rung — including rungs where only
  the wide window was positionally sufficient. Channel count is a
  real price the sense pays before its information helps (0115;
  the same reading at every rung and meter).
- **The competence envelope binds, not the encoding.** Beyond short
  dependencies (T ≈ 8 consecutive-correct at this configuration),
  behavior plateaus budget-invariantly (0.53–0.68 violations/step
  vs random's exact 0.750; 2× budget moved it ≤ 0.003) regardless
  of encoding capacity, and every meter dies with the behavior:
  acceptance-style meters demand fidelity exponential in sequence
  length; anticipation probes are behavior-coupled and arbitrate
  only at comparable competence (0115; Q1 inversion + Amendment 1
  trail).

## Design guidance for the teacher-world experiment

1. **The sense:** declare a cheap, narrow, structure-matched
   encoding — a small window for positional/phase structure, a
   decay summary where counting or recency matters, both only if
   measurement demands it. Do not buy width speculatively; it
   costs before it pays.
2. **The curriculum:** keep taught structures inside the measured
   competence envelope until the envelope itself moves; sequence
   length, not vocabulary or encoding, is the current frontier.
3. **The meters:** never gate on all-or-nothing completion of long
   sequences (exp(T) fidelity demand); use graded conformity
   against exact floors/ceilings, and use anticipation probes only
   between arms of comparable behavior.

## The attribution — ANSWERED (episode 0116)

**The envelope is shared, and the frames hold the wall.** The
discriminating registration ran the day after this document named
it: the drive's misalignment was real and fixable — the completion
itch mapped to the world's own progress channel, WITHOUT
commitment, beats the plain drive inside the envelope (+32%
acceptance at R(2)) and moves every interior meter beyond it
(conformity −0.10 to −0.17 at every edge rung) — and with the
misalignment repaired, the wall stands (R(4) acceptance 1.7× plain,
still under the frozen line; R(8) at the deepest conformity in the
record with not one full sequence). What remains when the drive
wants to finish and cannot is representational: the compositional
gate is measured (0042's prediction confirmed at its named
location) — and **shaped by the owner's direction (episode 0117) as
references between frames**, hierarchy an emergent special case,
never an imposed architecture. What a reference carries (pose,
prediction, learned binding) is the open core of that research; the
lifecycle costs (credit through references, dangling references at
eviction, topology in the snapshot, pricing references in the
survival score) are named in 0117 for the registration that picks
it up.

Two mechanism lessons ride with the attribution (0116, measured by
ablation): **incumbency taxes token switches** — commitment as
shipped (dying with its intention) punishes exactly what sequence
worlds require at boundaries; a *boundary-releasing hold* is the
sequence-commitment shape a successor must have. And **the itch
without incumbency is a free win for sequence worlds**, licensed
for the teacher-world's bounded-context configuration.

## Constraints carried forward

machinery; instrument bars before behavior bars; margins against
measured ceilings and exact floors (the 0112 lesson, applied
