# Episode 0069 — Goal homing: presence solved, election isolated (2026-08-08)

The parked self-set-goals ladder reopened through its own named door —
E2.0b, the homing-capable goal term — on the owner's pick, the same day
the c1c close (episode 0068) removed the drives-just-need-time
alternative. One question, registered before any runner existed: is
gradient reach the missing piece, or is the wall deeper? The gate
swapped exactly one term in the twice-refuted `GoalBiasPolicy`: the
measurably-flat obs-distance became a clone-step oracle potential
(ground-truth Chebyshev distance to the workshop, deliberately the
upper bound of anything learnable).

**The instrument rebuild earned its own lesson first.** Attempt 1 used
the Config default `weight_norm_cap = 0.0` and matched every coarse
anchor — graduate cycles/frames/pred-samples exact against the 034
pilot, dwell median in band, 0/24 over 20% — while producing zero
chains `[measured]`. The registered HARD criterion (the chain set
admits no variants) did exactly its job: nothing downstream was read.
The c1c-posture cap (1.2, arc 026) was the diagnosis, confirmed by a
two-chain fingerprint: seed 6 @ λ0.25 → tick 706 and seed 7 @ λ4 →
tick 427, both recorded chains at their exact recorded ticks
`[measured]`. Coarse anchors are not identity; the record's exact-tick
criteria are.

**The verdicts, at 24-seed power** (pilot published first, per
protocol; Bar 2 amended openly by the owner *before* the confirmatory
when the pilot showed its premise had evaporated):

- **Bar 1 — holding: PASS 24/24** (bar ≥ 18/24 at ≥ 20% dwell),
  median dwell **99.98%** against the obs-form's recorded 3.8%
  `[measured]`.
- **Bar 2 — position control: PASS** in its amended never-leaves
  form: **0 departures in 24 runs** `[measured]`. E2.0's leak (ε-noise
  walking the runner off the plateau, never to return) is closed by
  removal.
- **Bar 3 — chains: FAIL 0/24** (bar ≥ 6/24). 120,000 steps standing
  at the workshop, the taught chain provably in the frames, and not
  one log dug (3 cobblestone across the arm; F control 0 chains,
  G-λ4 in its P0 band) `[measured]`. Against the 2-in-42 floor,
  0/24 alone is not significant (Fisher p ≈ 0.5) — the finding is the
  direction: **~25× presence bought zero chain gain.**

**Refuted:** the author's frozen prediction, for the third gate
running — it predicted high return rates and a narrow Bar-3 pass; the
oracle instead abolished departures entirely and chained nowhere.
Kept as calibration data. Also refuted, at its strongest form: the
hope that position-wanting alone converts knowledge into behavior.

**What it taught / opened:** the registered decision rule's middle
branch fires — the far gradient stands as a measured *component*
(presence is now free, at every λ, with zero escapes), and **election
is the isolated wall**: choosing dig₁…dig₁₂ at a place the frontier
drive scores as exhausted, against ε-interruption, with nothing that
values finishing `[mechanism-argument]`. The E2.1 mechanism decision
(rollouts / means-ends / skills) is the owner's, gated before any
build. The same evening's owner conversation assembled the wider map —
motivation as a stack of layers (budget, deficits, options, completion
itch, borrowed goals, imagination), each patching the ugly twin of the
one below, with the record's five nulls mapped as single upper layers
tested over a missing floor — carried forward as the successor topic
`hq/01-RESEARCH/motivation-stack/`, which orders five candidate gates
and registers none `[judgment]`.

Reversal condition: the component claim reopens if a later gate shows
repeated chains with gradient reach absent but position otherwise
controlled (the gradient would be over-credited), or if any
