# Episode 0079 — The provisioned life: the composition endures (2026-08-09 → 2026-08-10)

Every pass in the goals arc was a burst: twenty-four fresh brains, fifty
thousand steps each, one mechanism per gate. c1d-lab asked the question
none of them could: does the measured stay/want/finish composition
**endure** — one brain, one continuous world, no resets, no ground
truth anywhere in the loop, the meter always on? The life was
pre-registered before launch
([`C1D-LAB-RUN-PLAN.md`](../02-DESIGN/validate/C1D-LAB-RUN-PLAN.md)):
the G4 meter-cohort graduate (seed 1) running frames' drive + the
brain-side hold (Doc 0009) + the completion itch (κ = 0.25, λ = 0.25),
under the owner's tapered childhood (G4b's measured dose, one weaning
at the start of life) in a world that regrows a dug column 2,000 ticks
after digging.

It ended by the pre-registered goal rule, as openly amended before it
fired: **75,359 full chains at 25,003,001 steps** [measured] — the
chain bar cleared 37× over, stopped at the amended halfway gate. The
five registered readings, all [measured] over 500 per-segment rows:

- **R1 — endurance:** chains per 5M-step fifth 15,187 / 15,540 /
  15,298 / 14,518 / 14,816 — flat (spread 6.8% of the mean, and the
  final fifth *rose*). Election neither decayed nor grew. Not one of
  the 500 segments was chainless.
- **R2 — hold drift:** dwell 99.97–99.98% in every fifth. The
  brain-side hold does not drift over a lifetime; episode 0074's

Nothing was refuted. What it taught: the composition that was assembled

any of its parts wearing out [measured]. The registered horizon was

unmeasured evidence space, said plainly. Ops honesty lives in the run
