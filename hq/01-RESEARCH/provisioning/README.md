# Provisioning — can the childhood dose end by demonstration instead of by date?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-11

## Abstract

The childhood stipend (G4b's taper) is a hand-picked calendar: grace until
tick A, weaned by tick B. It was tuned to the lab and had to be re-dosed by
hand for reality (c1e amendment debate; the corpse-line arithmetic). The
owner's direction: magic numbers the brain — or here, the parent — should
figure out. This topic asks whether provisioning can be **event-gated**:
the stipend ends because the child demonstrably feeds itself, not because a
date arrived. A decisive pass retires the wean date everywhere the meter
goes; a decisive fail records that the dose is genuinely parental judgment.

## The question

Can one provisioning rule, with no calendar dates and no per-world retuning,
keep taught children alive across two economies whose income costs differ
threefold — where the fixed-taper dose demonstrably fails to transfer?

## Pre-registered bars

The bars constrain any candidate rule; the pilot may explore rule forms
(shortfall coverage until sustained competence, matching grants, finite
purse) — the confirmatory arms run ONE frozen rule. Time-constant-class
quantities (a trailing-income measurement window) are permitted and declared;
calendar wean dates and per-economy constants are not.

- **Bar P1 (adequacy):** on the measured sample-field meter world
  (economy 1), taught seeds alive at horizon 20,000 ≥ 22/24, and no more
  than 2 below the recorded fixed-taper reference arm run alongside.
- **Bar P2 (no infinite welfare):** untaught floor seeds (8) under the same
  rule: alive at horizon ≤ 2/8, and mean stipend paid to a floor seed
  < 2× the mean paid to a taught seed. A rule that carries the incompetent
  forever fails, whatever else it does.
- **Bar P3 (transfer — the point):** the SAME rule with the SAME constants
  on a 3×-taxed economy (acquisition costs tripled, mirroring reality's
  measured dig cost): taught alive ≥ 22/24, while the economy-1-tuned fixed
  taper loses ≥ 6 more seeds there than it lost in economy 1. If the fixed
  dose transfers fine, the premise collapses — record it and abandon.

## Reversal condition

If every rule form that passes P1+P2 needs its constants re-tuned to pass
P3, provisioning cannot be event-gated for this composition — the dose is
parental judgment, the calendar stays, and the topic returns to the owner
with the measured forms and where each broke.

## Verdict

- **Bars P1/P2 — shapes held at pilot** `[measured]`: the
  demonstration gate beat the calendar on the measured economy (8/8
  alive, weans 1023–1567 vs the hand date 3,000, cheaper stipends);
  the purse amendment fixed infinite welfare (floor seeds 0/4, purses
  exhausted — as designed); the un-recalibrated fixed dose failed the
  taxed economy exactly as c1e's corpse lines predicted.
- **Bar P3 — unfalsifiable in the lab, then MOOT** `[measured]`: the
  recalibrated economy erased the expression delay a childhood dose
  exists to bridge (both rules survive 8/8), and the topic paused for
  re-grounding in a native world. Native-survival then retired the
  ENTIRE stipend layer (episode 0103): the blessed stack's lives are
  born hungry with no parental dose and first-eat unaided within ~2k
  steps — there is no synthetic wean to gate.
- Outcome: **abandoned — the premise dissolved, and that is the
  result.** What stands as parental design wisdom, recorded: the
  demonstration gate beats the calendar where both exist; patience
  is a purse, not a date; a dose exists only where an expression
  delay does.
