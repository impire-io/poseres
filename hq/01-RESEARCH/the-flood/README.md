# The flood — can a deficit that floods perception initiate the chain a quiet one cannot?

**State:** active
**Started:** 2026-08-13

## Abstract

Distal-senses measured the initiation wall: a starving body two
visible blocks from food forages in ~1 of 3 lives, triggered by
exploration, never by hunger — because hunger reaches action selection
only inside completions and recipe choice, a whisper at the END of
chains. The owner's design intuition, registered here: real hunger is
not a quiet linear channel. It *floods* — inaudible at first, then
present, then most of what perception is, "up to the point where you
can't think about anything more than solving the hunger." This topic
builds the flood as a SENSE (the missing sense is urgency itself) and
asks whether it reaches initiation. It is layer 2 of the owner's
motivation stack — deficits as priority signals — given its second,
unshipped half.

## The old-brain fence (owner's scope rule, 2026-08-13)

Floodable senses in humans come from the old brain, not the neocortex
— which might warrant special cases in processing. **Watched
carefully:** in pra, the flood therefore lives at the BODY seam (the
observation the world hands the brain), beneath the cortical machinery
(frames, head, drives), which stays generic and untouched. Brain-side
special-casing is OUT OF SCOPE for this topic; if both body-side forms
fail their bars, brain-side machinery becomes a named successor
needing its own registration — never a quiet amendment here.

## The question

With the deficit expanded nonlinearly into the observation — the flood
— does a hungry-born life initiate the taught forage chain where the
quiet-channel body measurably did not (expression 1/6 at the D3
protocol, 0/3 gated), while releasing perception when sated?

## Scope, declared

- **The flood curve**: f(d) = 0 for deficit d ≤ θ, else
  ((d − θ)/(1 − θ))², with θ = 0.25 — silent above 15/20 food,
  swelling below, f = 1 at starvation. ONE dial point per form in the
  pilot; sweeps only if both forms are null (anti-tuning discipline).
- **Two body-side forms**, one channel (`flood`, width 4, survival
  instrument, appended last; obs 73 → 77):
  - **Intrusion**: [f, f·n₁, f·n₂, f·n₃] where n_i is a
    deterministic per-game-tick pseudo-noise (signature-style hash of
    the world's own clock) — a swelling, unpredictable signal.
    Mechanism story, stated before the run: the noise raises realized
    prediction error wherever f is high; the drives already prefer
    predictable futures; the head learns that eating lowers f (dim 0
    is smooth); relief becomes valuable through the EXISTING pipeline
    — the drives cannot smell calories (episode 0082) but they can
    hear the flood.
  - **Gain (sensitization)**: [f, f·drops_present, f·(1 − drops_dist),
    f·hand_edible] — hunger amplifies food cues into a dedicated loud
    channel, zero when sated or cueless. Classifier-free constraint,
    named: only the world's own edibility fact and ground items
    qualify as cues; the glance CANNOT contribute without naming food
    (033). Known weakness, stated: amplifying silence when no cue is
    in view.
- **The ablation body**: same anatomy (obs 77), flood mode off →
  the channel reads zeros. Same taught brain runs flooded and
  silenced — the honest F3 pair.
- **The laundering hazard, named**: the channel-quality estimator
  down-weights noisy channels by design; if enabled it may mute the
  intrusion. Recorded as reading R-launder (estimator off in the
  current BASE; verified and stated with the pilot).
- Everything live-world (the GENESIS rule); the fake carries shape
  only. The full shipped composition (RecipePolicy + the 042 gate at
  κ_d = 0.1) runs in every arm — the flood is additive to it.
- Teaching: 45 sense-using lessons (the distal-senses curriculum)
  with the flood ON — the lessons must WITNESS the flood swelling
  with the dose and quieting as the tape eats.

## Pre-registered bars (live world only)

- **Bar F1 (initiation):** ≥ 4 of 6 flooded hungry-born lives (6,000
  steps, the D3 protocol) produce ≥ 1 genuine eat, with median
  first-eat < 3,000 steps. Measured floor: 1/6 lives, first-eats
  3,275/1,491 (distal-senses record).
- **Bar F2 (the flood releases — monomania bounded):** in every
  flooded life that reaches food ≥ 16, the following 1,000 steps show
  no single action above 50% share, `use_held` below 10% share, and
  ≥ 5 unique columns — sated perception is ordinary perception. The
  intended endpoint at the OTHER end is monomania (starving SHOULD
  crowd out everything); the bar is hysteresis, not absence.
- **Bar F3 (the flood carries it):** 6 flooded vs 6 silenced lives,
  same taught brain: flooded expression ≥ 4/6, silenced ≤ 2/6, gap
  ≥ 2 lives.
- **Reading R-form (pilot, published before the arms):** intrusion vs
  gain head-to-head at 3 lives each; the winning form runs the F
  bars. **R-launder** recorded alongside.

## Reversal condition

If neither form beats the measured no-flood floor at the pilot (and
the single-point dials are then swept once, openly, with the same
null), the premise — deficit-as-perceptual-occupation reaches
initiation — is refuted for this composition, and the topic returns
to the design conversation with the readings. If F1 passes but F2
fails at every dose, the flood needs a governor; that governor is new
machinery and needs its own registration. Brain-side special-casing
is never this topic's fallback (the old-brain fence).
