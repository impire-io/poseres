# Front and back matter scripts

Recorded as separate files. Retail distributors generally require opening
credits at the head of the first file and closing credits at the tail of the
last, or as standalone files — check the current spec before delivery.

Anything in `«guillemets»` is a placeholder the author must fill. **Do not
record with placeholders in place.** They are marked rather than guessed
because this book's own style guide bans inventing details, and a credit
sequence is the one place a listener will notice.

## A NOTE BEFORE WE START

Record this. It is short, it solves two real problems, and it is the only
place where the audio edition can explain how it differs from the book.

> A word about this recording.
>
> The book it comes from has two layers. The story you are about to hear is
> one of them, and it is complete on its own. The other layer is a set of
> technical asides — the mathematics, the failure data, the exact
> measurements — printed in boxes that a reader can skip. «CHOOSE ONE:
>
> — for the boxes-omitted edition: They are not in this recording. Nothing
> in the story depends on them. If you want them, they are in the text
> edition, and they are worth your time.
>
> — for the boxes-included edition: They are in this recording, read in a
> quieter voice. When you hear it change, that is an aside starting. You can
> stop listening for a minute without losing the thread.»
>
> The book also asks you, in part four, to install the system and watch it
> learn on your own screen. The commands for that are in the text edition and
> at «URL». You do not need them to follow the story.
>

## What is deliberately not here

**No dedication, acknowledgements, or author bio.** None exist in the
manuscript, and this file will not invent them. If you want them, write them
and add them here.

**No table of contents read aloud.** Sixteen chapter titles read in sequence
is thirty seconds a listener will skip, and every retail player shows the
chapter list on screen.

**No summary or "what you will learn".** STYLE.md bans chapter-final recaps
and section summaries on the grounds that the reader was there. A front-matter
summary is the same move, earlier.
