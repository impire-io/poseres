## The diagnostic (1,500 steps, per-frame trace)

- at-melon frames: 389 — **all 389 delegated to the head** (the decree
  yields correctly at one block).
- head dig frames at the melon: 211; **longest consecutive dig run:
  29 frames; mining progress max: 0.97; digs completed: 0; eats: 0.**
- decree frames breaking a held dig: **4** — the decree is not the
  interrupter. The head releases its own dig at ~97%, every time, and
  a released dig resets the block's damage: the last 3% is never paid.

The melon's live break time at the 50 ms fabric is ~30 consecutive
steps; the lessons taught with a 40-step dig tape. The head's
reproduced hold is ~29 — systematically one frame short, and vanilla
resets block damage on release, so near-misses pay nothing. In the
free-roaming arms, occasional eats (A2 13, A3 29) came from the
variance of longer holds; parked in the decree's exact one-block
geometry, the under-hold is deterministic and fatal.

## What this answers — and what it re-opens

The registered discriminator asked: does hand-wired aiming clear A2?
The honest answer is that the question dissolved one layer down. The
decree **created contact reliably** — position, the flood era's
"spatial luck" residual, is solved by steering on the palate's prices
— and expression STILL failed, inside the skill itself. The
reversal's second branch fires with a mechanism attached: **the gap
is not aim.** Below the aiming question sits a prior defect — the
taught dig cannot finish against the live world's own completion
timing when the geometry stops flattering it. Running the remaining
five control lives would have measured that defect five more times;
the control was stopped after the diagnostic, recorded here.

Successor question, named for registration (owner's call): **the last
crack** — why does the recipe's hold release at ~97% of the live dig,
and what closes the final frames (longer taught holds? completion
keyed to the world's own break — the drop — rather than the sensed
progress estimate?). Every arc reading since c1e that scored "the
skill expresses with variance" inherits a correction from this
measurement.
