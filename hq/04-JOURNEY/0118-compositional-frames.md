# Episode 0118 — Compositional frames: nothing a reference carries beats the tower — the reversal fires by its own condition (2026-08-24 → 2026-08-25)

The research 0117 commissioned, run to its registered verdict in a
day: what must a reference between frames carry — pose, prediction,
a learned binding — for a frame web to beat a plain stacked-frame
tower on the frozen echo-teacher world, same budget, same meters,
the 0116 protocol? Bars registered before any run: the tower
measured first (T0), a reference must beat it where the wall lives
(R0), the carried content answered head-to-head (W0), the lifecycle
payable (L0), the frozen 2.0 line reported separately (R1).

The scaffolding taught the first lesson before any bar read
[measured, pilot rows in the rig]: composition-as-input
(`aug_obs = [obs, z]`) died on its own instruments — every loss
diluted across the carrier channels, the kernel's lr scale rule
treating carrier as world, and the arbiter never engaging (≤ 0.5%
share at any setting), so no mechanism could express itself. The
seam that works is the **transition**: a tier-2 frame identical to a
base frame except its per-action transition reads `[own pose, z]` —
another frame's state as a constituent of this frame's dynamics,
0112's interaction lesson relocated. With it, both tiers compete on
the kernel's own survival score and the mechanism expresses itself
(arbiter share 0.2–0.7).

The verdicts [measured; 24 seeds, five rungs, rig committed before
removal]: **T0 PASS** — the tower is a real rival, R(4) mean 1.109
(rig median 1.231, 24/24 accepting) over flat's 0.878 (0.769,

ahead. **W0 answered**: what a reference carries does not matter at
this world and scale — pose vs prediction sit inside spread with

(+0.244 at R(4), t = +1.49). **R1 FAIL everywhere** — no arm's
median clears the frozen 2.0 line, R(8) is exactly zero in every
arm, and 0116's attribution stands untouched. **L0 mooted** by R0's
FAIL, its incidental dangling telemetry the partial answer 0117's
lifecycle question receives.

Reversed: 0117's shape, by its own pre-registered condition — the
simpler tower wins on parsimony and the gate reverts to
hierarchy-as-built. The reversal is the decision working as
designed, one day from shape to measurement.

What it taught: **composition pays as capacity on this world, not as
memory** — the echo world's progress channel hands sequence position
to the senses, leaving the observation nearly Markov-sufficient
given the current template, and template knowledge lives in fast
weights, not carried state [mechanism-argument]. The tower's
interior shift over flat (+0.231 at R(4), +0.256 at C(4), t ≈ 1.6
each) is consistent and suggestive, not established. And the
compositional gate itself remains open: nothing tested — flat,
stacked, or referencing — clears the wall.

Reversal condition: a reference mechanism beating the stacked tower
beyond seed spread on a world that *hides* sequence position (no
progress channel — state must be carried, not sensed) reopens
references as the gate's shape; a stacked configuration clearing
R(4)'s frozen 2.0 line on the frozen world closes the gate as
capacity-bought and reopens the envelope ladders per 0116's own
reversal.
